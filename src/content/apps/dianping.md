# Dianping (大众点评)

Dianping (大众点评, literally "public reviews") is China's dominant restaurant and lifestyle review platform — often described as the Chinese equivalent of Yelp or TripAdvisor for dining. It is owned by Meituan (美团) and is deeply integrated into the Meituan ecosystem.

## Key Facts

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Chinese name | 大众点评 |
| Parent company | Meituan |
| Platform | iOS, Android, Web, WeChat Mini Program |
| Works without VPN | Yes |
| Languages | Primarily Chinese; partial English support in major cities |
| Primary use | Restaurant discovery, reviews, ratings |

## Why Foreigners Use It

- **Restaurant discovery:** Star ratings, photo menus, and user-submitted food photos are navigable without reading Chinese.
- **Integrated with Amap:** Searching for restaurants in Amap (高德地图) pulls Dianping reviews and ratings directly into map results.
- **Vegetarian/halal search:** Search "素食" (vegetarian) or "清真" (halal) to find restaurants catering to dietary needs.
- **Price transparency:** Listings show average per-person cost (人均消费), helping set budget expectations.
- **Queue and reservation:** Some restaurants support queueing (排队) or table reservation (订座) through Dianping.

## How to Access

1. **WeChat Mini Program:** Search "大众点评" in WeChat — no separate download needed.
2. **Standalone app:** Search "Dianping" or "大众点评" in App Store / Google Play.
3. **Via Amap:** When searching for food in Amap, restaurant results link directly to Dianping reviews.

## Practical Tips for Foreign Visitors

- **Photo menus are your friend:** Even without reading Chinese, browse the photo section of any restaurant listing to see what dishes look like and what other diners ordered.
- **Rating system:** Dianping uses a 5-star scale — 4.0+ is decent, 4.5+ is very good, and restaurants below 3.5 are best avoided.
- **"Must-order" lists (必点菜):** Many restaurant pages have a section showing the most popular dishes — useful for deciding what to try.
- **Translation:** Use WeChat's built-in translate (tap and hold text) or take a screenshot and use a translation app to read reviews.
- **Location-based:** Allow location permissions for accurate nearby results.

## Limitations

- Interface is predominantly Chinese — requires basic navigation skills or translation tools.
- Account creation requires a phone number (Chinese or foreign accepted).
- Reviews are in Chinese — use translation tools to read them.
- Some features (coupons, group buying) require Chinese payment methods.
