# Finding venues with Dianping (大众点评)

## What is Dianping useful for?

Shanghai’s municipal portal describes Dianping (大众点评) as a customer review app. Use reviews to shortlist places, then check the address, opening hours and any dietary questions directly with the venue.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-BarsandPubs/20240227/fbb5411eab8b489ea719f013131d7c23.html)

## How can I explain a destination to a driver?

Canada’s travel advisory recommends carrying the destination and the name and address of your hotel written in Chinese characters. Ask your hotel for its Chinese address and keep a copy you can show without a connection.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china)

## Where can I find restaurant recommendations in English in Shanghai?

Try Dianping (大众点评) for restaurant discovery. Shanghai documented a specific Shanghai Must Eat List with food recommendations in English, Chinese and Korean. That language coverage applies to the list.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/cmsres/46/46fb01aff21a41d48bd4ae57be8bbda9/3e350b415280adf5ba42fe7ee7ca866e.pdf) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-ShanghaiWeeklyBulletin/20250709/d2cee37808e448d5b3e5ac0a14f7ed6a.html)

## Travel tip — Choosing a restaurant branch

Look at recent dish photos and the address together. Save the Chinese name of the dish you want to show the server. Check the branch on a map before walking over.

## Where can I download Dianping (大众点评) on iPhone?

Look for Dianping (大众点评) in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id351091731)

## Travel tip — Reading a restaurant menu

Save a dish name or photo you like, then ask the restaurant whether it is on today’s menu. Confirm the current price, opening hours and any reservation or voucher restrictions directly.

## Where can I find the Android app for Dianping (大众点评)?

The linked Google Play listing is for Dianping. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dianping.v1)

## How do I find vegetarian or halal restaurants on Dianping?

Use Dianping (大众点评) to look for places and try 素食 (vegetarian) or 清真 (halal) with the neighbourhood name. Confirm the actual dishes and preparation with staff. Shanghai describes halal-food identification separately; a user review alone does not establish suitability.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/cmsres/46/46fb01aff21a41d48bd4ae57be8bbda9/3e350b415280adf5ba42fe7ee7ca866e.pdf) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-LocalRules/20260924/ea0bcdb1a46f4a78be38bab289cdb9ca.html)

## Travel tip — Choosing a dish once you have found the restaurant

Open the specific branch and look at food photos and recent reviews. Look for 推荐菜 (recommended dishes) or 必点 (must-order) if your version shows those labels; you can also show staff a picture and ask 招牌菜是什么？ (What is your signature dish?). The annual 必吃榜 is a restaurant list, so check the branch and year on the listing.

[Meituan’s Dianping restaurant list](https://www.meituan.com/news/NN260625162008376)

## Travel tip — Find restaurant vouchers and set meals

In the Dianping (大众点评) app, open the restaurant branch you plan to visit and look for 团购 (deals), 套餐 (set meal) or 代金券 (cash-value voucher). Before buying, check which branch accepts it, the valid dates, what is included and whether you need a reservation. Check the payment options shown at checkout.

[Dianping user terms](https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/11?activeRule=1) · [Meituan and Dianping dining rules](https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/486?activeRule=1)

## Travel tip — Reading a restaurant listing when choosing a dish

If a listing shows 必点菜 (recommended dishes), compare the photos with the menu at the actual branch. For a dish you want to try, save the photo and the Chinese name to show your server. A review score alone tells you little about spice level, portion size or ingredients.
