# Meituan (美团) for food delivery, hotel booking and shared bikes

## How do I start a Meituan food order?

Shanghai’s guide describes SMS verification for a Meituan account. Have access to your phone for the code, enter the delivery address and check the contact details before ordering.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20240109/89844b5729d64b199463a156bc8ef43d.html)

## How can I order a meal in Shanghai?

Shanghai’s guide describes ordering on Meituan (美团) and Taobao Instant Commerce (淘宝闪购). Choose a delivery address, review the order and check the total before paying. Ask your accommodation where deliveries can be collected.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20240109/89844b5729d64b199463a156bc8ef43d.html)

## What will Meituan ask for when I place a delivery order?

Shanghai’s guide shows sign-up with a six-digit SMS code. Review your basket and address, choose whether you need cutlery and select an available payment method. The guide lists Alipay, WeChat Pay and the digital RMB wallet.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/bcc05ccb6de7460e89dc3294b5fbb755.html)

## Which button submits a Meituan food order?

After checking the items, delivery address and total, look for 提交订单, meaning “submit order”. Shanghai’s guide says Meituan will also ask whether you need cutlery.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/bcc05ccb6de7460e89dc3294b5fbb755.html)

## Can I follow my Meituan delivery after I order?

Shanghai’s guide says Meituan shows the estimated wait and lets you track the delivery driver after placing an order.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/bcc05ccb6de7460e89dc3294b5fbb755.html)

## What can I use Meituan for besides food delivery?

In Shanghai, Meituan (美团) is one option for booking accommodation. You can also use its app for yellow Meituan shared bikes (美团单车). Look for the bike operator shown on the bicycle. Compare actual room rates and cancellation terms for your dates if booking a hotel.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Transportation-Rideabike/20260813/3b4e059a25004d5a97814d4af9f283a2.html)

## How do I find a restaurant in the Meituan app?

Open 外卖 (food delivery) to browse nearby restaurants or search by name. Open a restaurant, choose dishes and review the basket before tapping 提交订单 (submit order).

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20240109/89844b5729d64b199463a156bc8ef43d.html)

## Travel tip — Receiving food at a hotel

Copy the hotel’s Chinese address from your confirmation. Ask reception which entrance, shelf or collection point to use and add that detail to the delivery note. Use a contact number you can answer.

## Where can I download Meituan (美团) on iPhone?

Look for Meituan (美团) in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id423084029)

## Where can I find the Android app for Meituan (美团)?

The linked Google Play listing is for Meituan. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sankuai.meituan)

## Can I use Meituan to look for attraction or show tickets?

Shanghai’s guide to exhibitions and shows lists the Meituan app and the WeChat mini program 美团景点游玩. For a particular performance or museum, check the venue’s own booking channel, admission rules and availability.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html)

## What is Meituan used for?

Meituan (美团) offers food delivery. In Shanghai, visitor guides also list Meituan for booking hotels, unlocking its yellow shared bikes and finding attraction tickets. Check availability for the specific hotel, bike or venue.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20240109/89844b5729d64b199463a156bc8ef43d.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Transportation-Rideabike/20260813/3b4e059a25004d5a97814d4af9f283a2.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html)

## Is Meituan cheaper than Trip.com for hotels?

Shanghai lists Meituan and Ctrip as hotel booking channels. Compare the same room, dates, breakfast and cancellation terms on the actual listings. There is no verified fixed saving in the cited guide.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html)
