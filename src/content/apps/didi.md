# Requesting a ride with DiDi (滴滴)

## How can I request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce lists Alipay, WeChat and DiDi as channels for ordering a ride. Set the pick-up point and destination and check the vehicle and fare shown for your trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## How can I explain a destination to a driver?

Canada’s travel advisory recommends carrying the destination and the name and address of your hotel written in Chinese characters. Ask your hotel for its Chinese address and keep a copy you can show without a connection.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china)

## Can I use an international phone number to request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce guide describes registering in the DiDi Travel app with an international phone number and bank card. Confirm the pickup and drop-off locations, then choose the service offered for that trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## How can I check that my DiDi is the right car and contact the driver?

When the driver accepts, compare the number plate shown in the app with the car before getting in. You can call or message the driver; the app offers message translation. The driver may ask you to confirm the last four digits of the mobile number used for the booking.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## How do I change DiDi to English?

In DiDi (滴滴), open Me (我的), then Settings (设置), General (通用) and Language (语言). Choose English and confirm.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## Which service names might I see when ordering a DiDi ride?

A Chinese consular guide lists Express, Taxi, Premier and Luxe as options. Select a service available for your pickup point and check the price shown.

[kolkata.china-consulate.gov.cn](https://kolkata.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/zlgxw/202404/t20240411_11280092.htm)

## Travel tip — Finding your driver

Check the pickup floor, zone and pillar number in the app, especially at an airport or station. Send the driver the sign text if you cannot find one another. Compare the vehicle and plate shown before getting in.

## Where can I download DiDi China (滴滴出行) on iPhone?

Look for DiDi China (滴滴出行) in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id554499054)

## Where can I find the Android app for DiDi China (滴滴出行)?

The linked Google Play listing is for DiDi China: Ride Hailing. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sdu.didi.psnger)

## Where do I meet a ride at Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station?

At Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station, follow the floor markings to the e-hailing pickup point shown for your car. One pickup area is on the first basement level of P10. Check the floor and location on your ride before walking to the car.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20260802/f314cad958d4426f832031a1c87fe1e9.html)

## How do I pay for DiDi?

Shanghai’s guide describes booking DiDi through WeChat or Alipay. For the ride booked through the wallet, the fare is deducted from that wallet after the trip. Check the price and payment method shown in your own booking.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## What if the DiDi driver can’t find me?

Open the ride details to check the driver’s location and contact them. Shanghai’s guide describes calling the driver or using in-app messages with English translation. Send the exact pickup zone or sign and compare the plate before getting in.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## Travel tip — Being ready for your pickup

Walk to the assigned pickup point before your car arrives when it is safe to do so. At a busy airport or station, compare the floor and zone in the app with the signs, then message your position if the driver is waiting somewhere else.
