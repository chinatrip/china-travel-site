# Requesting a ride with DiDi (滴滴)

## How can I request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce lists Alipay, WeChat and DiDi as channels for ordering a ride. Set the pick-up point and destination and check the vehicle and fare shown for your trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## How can I explain a destination to a driver?

Canada’s travel advisory recommends carrying the destination and the name and address of your hotel written in Chinese characters. Ask your hotel for its Chinese address and keep a copy you can show without a connection.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china)

## Can I use an international phone number to request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce guide describes registering in the DiDi Travel app with an international phone number and bank card. Confirm the pickup and drop-off locations, then choose the service offered for that trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## How can I check that my DiDi is the right car and contact the driver?

When the driver accepts, compare the number plate shown in the app with the car before getting in. You can call or message the driver; the app offers message translation. The driver may ask you to confirm the last four digits of the mobile number used for the booking.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## How do I change DiDi to English?

In DiDi (滴滴), open Me (我的), then Settings (设置), General (通用) and Language (语言). Choose English and confirm.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/4e46975d38f148cb9cf830021d349c46.html)

## Which service names might I see when ordering a DiDi ride?

A Chinese consular guide lists Express, Taxi, Premier and Luxe as options. Select a service available for your pickup point and check the price shown.

[kolkata.china-consulate.gov.cn](https://kolkata.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/zlgxw/202404/t20240411_11280092.htm)
