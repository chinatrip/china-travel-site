# 12306 Train Tickets: Passports, Changes and Refunds

**12306** is China Railway's official online ticketing service. Its English website supports purchases, ticket changes and refunds. Use the document details that match the passenger, and check the departure station as well as the city.

## Can I book with a foreign passport?

Yes. The [12306 English FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html) accepts valid foreign passports for its English website. Enter the passenger's name, document type and number accurately. Follow the identity-verification prompts; do not assume a guaranteed one- or two-day turnaround. The [12306 booking guidance](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/buyTicket.html) describes verification status and station-ticket-window alternatives.

Prepare your account early, then check the actual on-sale date/time and availability in 12306. If online verification fails, ask a station ticket window or 12306 for help rather than repeatedly changing your name or document number to force a match.

## How do I enter the station with an e-ticket?

Carry the original valid identity document used for the booking and retain the ticket information. Follow station instructions for document checks and use a staffed channel if needed. An itinerary printout or reimbursement receipt is **not** a boarding ticket. Do not assume a QR code alone or every passport scanner will work. Source: [12306 e-ticket and identity FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html).

## How do changes and refunds differ?

**改签 (ticket change)** changes your travel date, train or seat/berth. **换乘** means transferring between trains, not changing a ticket. The English service page gives different cutoffs for a ticket change, a destination change and a refund; check the transaction offered for your ticket and available seats. Source: [12306 change/refund service rules](https://www.12306.cn/en/train-tickets.html).

For a ticket purchased on 12306 and not checked in/used, request an ordinary online refund **before departure**. Electronic payments normally return to the original payment method. For an eligible cash purchase refunded online, take the identity document used to buy the ticket to a designated station window within 180 days of the online refund to collect the money. For trips from 1 October 2025, railway reimbursement uses electronic invoices. Source: [12306 online refund rules](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/onlineRefund.html), [China Railway passenger rules](https://mobile.12306.cn/otsmobile/h5/otsbussiness/info/transportationRules.html) and [State Taxation Administration invoice guidance](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100015/c5253362/content.html).

## What are the ordinary domestic refund fees?

| Time before the ticket's departure | Fee |
|---|---|
| 8 days or more (inclusive) | 0% |
| 48 hours to less than 8 days | 5% |
| 24 hours to less than 48 hours | 10% |
| Less than 24 hours | 20% |

The operator also sets rounding and a minimum fee where a fee applies. Rebooked tickets can have exceptions: moving a ticket from inside eight days to beyond eight days does not necessarily make a later refund free. Tickets changed after departure are not ordinarily refundable. Follow the system's actual quoted fee and the relevant special-service rules. Source: [12306 refund conditions](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/windowRefund.html).

## What should I check before paying?

Confirm the date, exact station, train, seat/berth, passenger document and total price. Use a payment method offered in that transaction; a particular foreign card or wallet is not guaranteed. If buying through an intermediary, check its separate service fee and change/refund terms. Do not treat a reseller's advance request as an issued ticket.

This guide uses **operator-primary evidence**, not immigration policy. It does not promise fixed train speeds, cheaper morning departures, particular onboard amenities or universal baggage allowances. Check the rules shown for your service. See [[wiki/concepts/transportation.md]] and [[wiki/entities/trip-com.md]] for related planning.

## Can I change my train or arrival station?

China Railway’s guide allows a ticket to be changed once. If you need to change the arrival station, apply at least 48 hours before departure. Once the arrival station has been changed, no further changes to that ticket’s date, train, seat class or arrival station are allowed.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Transportation/20240110/f69efb3643094385a0efeb62f594200f.html)

## Can I get a free refund if I bought the wrong train ticket?

For travel from 2 February 2026, registered 12306 users who paid electronically or with reward points on the official website, app or Alipay mini program may refund one eligible order per day free of charge. Process it within 30 minutes of purchase and at least four hours before departure. Domestic trains and services to or from Hong Kong West Kowloon qualify. Rebooked, multi-ride, periodic, pre-ordered, waitlisted, combined air/rail or water/rail, group and Railway e-Pass tickets are excluded.

[www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## How do I count the ordinary 15-day rail booking window?

Count the travel date as one of the 15 calendar dates. For example, tickets for 1 October 2026 went on sale on 17 September. Check your departure station’s release time and train availability on 12306.

[www.beijing.gov.cn](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/sy/jrts/202609/t20260915_4864621.html)

## Does a changed train ticket have a special Spring Festival refund rule?

If you change a ticket or change its destination and the new travel date falls in the Spring Festival travel period, the refund fee is 20% of the ticket price. Check this before confirming a change.

[english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/livinginbeijing/transportation/railway/202512/t20251226_4364929.html)

## What number can I call with a China Railway question?

China Railway’s customer service number is 12306. Its official website and app also have an English interface.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/services/investment/202602/04/content_WS69829975c6d00ca5f9a08e8a.html) · [english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/AssetsZi/A_Guide_to_Working_and_Living_in_China_as_Business_Expatriates_2025.pdf)

## What does China Railway ask passengers to avoid eating on the train?

Shanghai’s municipal district guide conveys China Railway’s advice on durian (榴莲) and luosifen noodles (螺蛳粉). You may carry these strong-smelling foods in sealed packaging. Do not eat them on the train.

[www.shhk.gov.cn](https://www.shhk.gov.cn/xwzx/002008/002008040/20230129/3d3d3854-1dcc-4eef-a475-b91b8e1dc6b9.html)

## Can I pay cash at a railway station or use a foreign bank card on 12306?

For tickets on the English 12306 website or app, the national guide lists bank cards issued in China or overseas and mobile payment. Station ticket counters also take RMB cash. Check the signs or ask the cashier which payment methods that counter supports.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025)

## Travel tip — Checking stations and connections

Compare the full Chinese station name on the booking and your map, including directions such as 北 (north) and 西 (west). Save it with the train number, date and departure time. Allow time to find the entrance, pass security and reach the gate. Verify changes against the station display.

## Travel tip — Testing your train search before booking

Try a route search before the day tickets go on sale. Compare the station names and train numbers, and check the live booking window for your travel date. Save a second time or route if the first choice sells out.

## Should I book trains on 12306 or Trip.com?

12306.cn and Railway 12306 are China Railway’s own sales channels. Trip.com is a booking intermediary. Compare the final total and the change and refund terms before choosing, and save the booking reference for the channel you use.

[www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html) · [www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/contents/service-guideline/terms.html?curr=QAR&locale=en-US)

## How can I tell which high-speed train seats are beside the window?

The National Railway Administration describes A and F as window positions in the layouts it lists. For example, the usual five-across layout uses A, B, C on one side and D, F on the other. Look at the coach and seat on the actual booking.

[www.nra.gov.cn](https://www.nra.gov.cn/ztzl/hy/kjcx/kpdd/201605/t20160526_145850.shtml) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/latest/news/202507/t20250721_4153469.html)

## Travel tip — Choose a hard or soft sleeper berth

On conventional trains, a hard sleeper (硬卧) has lower (下铺), middle (中铺) and upper (上铺) berths. A soft sleeper (软卧) has lower and upper berths. If climbing is difficult, look for a lower berth and check the assignment on the ticket. Compare the fares for your departure.

[China Railway berth options, via Shanghai Hongkou](https://www.shhk.gov.cn/xwzx/002008/002008040/20230611/5638be5d-8321-492d-86e1-7b880d1e45cd.html)

## Travel tip — Find a charging socket on the train

On MTR’s Vibrant Express (动感号), second class has power sockets in each row. If you cannot see one near your seat, ask the attendant: 请问充电插座在哪里？ (Where is the charging socket?) On other trains, check the facilities for your service.

[MTR Vibrant Express service guide](https://www.highspeed.mtr.com.hk/res/pdf/Service_Guide_EN.pdf)
