# 12306 for Foreigners: Book China Train Tickets (2026)

12306 is the official ticketing platform for China Railway and the only authoritative channel for high-speed, bullet, and conventional train tickets.

## Basics

- **Website**: https://www.12306.cn (English version available)
- **Hotline**: 12306 (English support available)
- **App**: App Store / Google Play; English UI
- **Booking window:** Tickets go on sale **15 days before departure**. Book as soon as the window opens for popular routes and holiday periods — popular G-train seats sell out quickly.

## Can foreigners use 12306?

- Registration requires a phone number (foreign numbers accepted) and a passport number.
- Tickets must be booked under your real passport details.
- E-tickets: scan the QR code at the turnstile — carry your **original passport** as the gate may require it for verification.
- Counter pickup: passport required.

## How do I pay on 12306?

- **Alipay, WeChat Pay** (recommended for foreign visitors — reliably accepted)
- UnionPay debit / credit
- Visa / Mastercard at the counter (acceptance varies; use Alipay/WeChat Pay as primary)
- RMB cash (counter only)

## What seat classes and train types does 12306 offer?

| Code | Type | Key seat classes |
|---|---|---|
| G | High-speed (350 km/h) | Second class, First class, Business |
| D | Bullet / intercity (250 km/h) | Second class, First class |
| Z/T/K | Conventional overnight | Seat, Hard sleeper (6-berth), Soft sleeper (4-berth) |

## What are 12306's refund and change rules?

Reissue (换乘): allowed before departure via app / website / counter; normally one reissue per ticket.

Refund fees (per the 12306 official refund policy, as of June 2026):

| Time before departure | Refund fee |
|---|---|
| More than 8 days | 0% (full refund) |
| 48 hours – 8 days | 5% |
| 24 – 48 hours | 10% |
| Less than 24 hours | 20% |

Refunds can be processed online up until roughly **30 minutes before** the train's scheduled departure (at a station counter, before departure); after departure tickets are generally non-refundable. Confirm the exact online cut-off at 12306.cn. These percentages apply to all train types; special cases can differ (e.g. Spring Festival peak-season rules). Note: for travel dates from Feb 2, 2026, 12306 also offers **one free wrong-ticket refund per calendar day** for an accidental purchase if requested within 30 minutes of payment and at least 4 hours before departure.

## What are the alternatives to 12306 for foreigners?

**Trip.com** and **Klook** offer English-language 12306 booking with customer support — useful if 12306's registration or payment step proves problematic. Both charge a service fee on top of the face-value ticket price. Book directly on 12306 to avoid this markup; the 12306 app and website have English support and are the recommended path for most visitors. (Source: Reddit r/travelchina, 2026-05-08)

## Practical Tips for Foreigners

- **Passport format rejection:** 12306 sometimes rejects foreign passport numbers during registration or booking. Common fix: ensure you enter the passport number exactly as printed (no spaces, all caps). If it still fails after multiple attempts, use **Trip.com** instead (~¥50 service fee per ticket, same train inventory, full English support, foreign cards accepted).
- **Seat selection limitation:** The English version of the 12306 app assigns seats randomly. Seat selection (window/aisle preference) is only available in the Chinese-language version. Trip.com allows seat preference indication.
- **Morning trains are often cheaper** than afternoon departures on the same route — book early slots to save money.
- **Choosing the right station:** Major cities have multiple rail stations (e.g., Beijing has Beijing West, Beijing South, Beijing North). Stations can be 30–60 minutes apart — always confirm which station your train departs from and plan transit time accordingly.
- **On-board amenities:** Free hot water dispensers on all trains (bring your own cup or buy one at the station). Snacks and meals are allowed on board. Dining cars available on most G/D trains. Snack carts pass through regularly.
- **Power banks on trains:** No CCC certification required (unlike domestic flights). Allowed as long as capacity is under 100 Wh (27,000 mAh) with clear labeling.
- **G vs D trains:** G trains (高铁) run at 300+ km/h and serve major city pairs. D trains (动车) are slightly slower (~250 km/h) but still fast and comfortable, often with more departure times available.
- **Phone charging:** Power outlets available at seats on most G/D trains (check near the floor or under the armrest).

## Service Highlights

- Nationwide station coverage with real-time seat availability
- E-tickets (QR scan at gate) and paper tickets supported
- Major stations (Grade 1 / 2 hub stations) offer staffed baggage storage and lost-and-found
- English announcements and signage at major hubs
