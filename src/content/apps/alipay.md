# Alipay for visitors to China

## How do I make a payment at a merchant?

The Ministry of Commerce guide describes two ways to pay by mobile wallet. Scan the merchant’s collection code, or show the payment code in your own wallet for the merchant to scan. Check the merchant and the amount before authorizing payment.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## What does it cost to use a linked overseas card?

For an eligible overseas card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, the Shanghai government guide says the platform fee is generally waived for a transaction of CNY 200 or less. Above CNY 200 a 3% fee may apply to the entire purchase. Check the amount shown at checkout and any separate fees from your issuer.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)

## What if Alipay or WeChat Pay cannot verify my foreign card?

Linking an overseas card requires verification by the card issuer. If verification fails, contact the issuer or the wallet’s customer service. Supported cards, limits and fees depend on the wallet. Check the information displayed for your own transaction.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## Do I need a mainland bank account, and how much can I spend?

Visitors can link an eligible overseas card to Alipay or Weixin Pay without opening a mainland Chinese bank account. Shanghai’s guide gives upper limits of US$5,000 per transaction and US$50,000 cumulatively per year. Check the limit shown for your own account in the app.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)

## Can I use a wallet from home to scan merchant QR codes?

China’s official guide lists participating overseas wallets including GCash from the Philippines, Kakao Pay from South Korea and TrueMoney from Thailand. Users can scan supported merchant QR codes with the wallet they already hold. Check the payment screen for the terms of your transaction.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/AssetsZi/A_Guide_to_Working_and_Living_in_China_as_Business_Expatriates_2025.pdf)

## Can I register Alipay before arriving in China?

Shanghai’s guide says you can register with an overseas mobile number before arrival. In the app, open Me, then Bank Cards, to add an eligible international card.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-PaymentsInShanghai/20260920/f22b7e0f606c407e9da9d26b089eac48.html)

## Can I link an overseas American Express card to Alipay?

Shanghai reported that eligible overseas American Express cards could be linked to Alipay from 25 February 2025. At launch, American Express cards issued by overseas partners were excluded. Check your own card’s eligibility in the app.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20250228/5af2c2cb1807459a84f5b49e6a1ebb06.html)

## How do I prepare a mobile wallet before shopping?

Install Alipay (支付宝) or WeChat (微信). Follow the registration and bank-card prompts. A foreign or Chinese mobile number can be used for registration. Your issuer verifies the card. At a merchant you can scan their collection code or show your payment code.

[english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/specials/aguidetoworkingandlivinginchinaasbusinessexpatriates2024/dailylifeservices/202404/t20240424_3635122.html)

## Travel tip — Checking a payment

Look at the merchant name, amount and currency before confirming. If the screen freezes, check the transaction record and ask the merchant whether payment arrived before trying again.

## Where can I download Alipay (支付宝) on iPhone?

Look for Alipay (支付宝) in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id333206289)

## Where can I find the Android app for Alipay (支付宝)?

The linked Google Play listing is for Alipay. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eg.android.AlipayGphone)

## Travel tip — Trying the wallet on a small purchase

Once your card is linked, try paying for something inexpensive that you already want, such as water. Check the merchant, amount and transaction record. This lets you practise before a larger purchase; a successful small payment only tells you that this particular transaction worked.
