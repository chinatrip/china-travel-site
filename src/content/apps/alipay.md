# How to Set Up Alipay in China as a Foreigner (2026)

Alipay (Ant Group) is one of China's two dominant mobile-payment platforms. It covers payments, lifestyle services, and transportation.

**Set up before you arrive** — SMS verification works on a foreign carrier; in-country setup is unreliable due to the GFW and a fresh Chinese SIM may not receive verification codes.

## How do foreigners set up Alipay?

1. Download the Alipay App (App Store / Google Play).
2. Register with a **foreign mobile number**.
3. Complete passport-based identity verification.
4. Under **Me → Bank Cards**, bind a Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club, or Discover card. American Express is also supported across China since February 2025 (overseas-issued cards; cards issued by certain local partners abroad may be excluded).
5. You can now scan to pay anywhere Alipay is accepted.

## What are Alipay's fees and limits for foreign cards?

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Single payment under ¥200 | 0% Alipay surcharge |
| Single payment ¥200 or over | **3%** surcharge on the full amount |
| Card FX fee | 1–3% (set by your bank, separate) |

PBOC regulatory cap: ~¥36,000 per transaction for verified accounts; ~¥360,000 annual. Check your current in-app limit after completing ID verification.

## What is Alipay Tour Pass, and do I need it?

> **Update (as of June 2026):** The original Tour Pass mini-program has been deprecated and is no longer promoted; multiple 2026 traveler guides report it has been replaced by direct card binding. As of 2024, Alipay allows foreigners to directly bind international credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club, Discover; American Express since February 2025) to their main Alipay account. Transaction limits follow the PBOC policy (see Fees and Limits above): a single transaction of up to USD 5,000 (~¥36,000) and an annual cumulative cap of USD 50,000 (~¥360,000) for verified accounts. The 'Tour Pass' and 'TourCard' product names are no longer the active method — direct card binding is the current approach.

**Tour Pass** (prepaid wallet) is **no longer necessary for most visitors** — direct card binding (added in 2024) is simpler and has higher limits. If the Alipay app shows a Tour Pass prompt during onboarding, dismiss it and use the standard Bank Cards flow instead.

Where Tour Pass is still encountered, it charged a **5% fee** per transaction (vs 3% for direct card binding) and auto-refunded unused balance to the original card after 90 days, with no fee on refunds. (Source: realchinaguide.com 2026)

**TourCard** (top-up balance variant) was a prepaid balance option within Alipay — load ¥500 at a time to minimize fee frequency, with top-ups taking ~10 seconds from a foreign card. (Source: realchinaguide.com 2026)

**Alipay+** allows payment via foreign e-wallets (GCash, Kakao Pay, GrabPay, TrueMoney, and others) — useful if you already use one of those apps.

## What can I do with Alipay?

- **Scan to pay / receive**: restaurants, supermarkets, attractions
- **Ride-hailing**: integrated DiDi entry
- **Food delivery**: integrated 淘宝闪购 / Taobao Instant Commerce (the Alibaba-owned Ele.me brand was retired and folded into Taobao Instant Commerce in late 2025; seamless Alipay integration)
- **Tickets**: flights, train, attractions
- **Bike-sharing**: Hello Bike (also shows bike options on Alipay homepage)
- **Metro mini-programs**: Access city metro QR codes (Yitongxing, Metro Daduhui, etc.) directly through Alipay

## Practical Tips (Source: realchinaguide.com 2026)

- **Download before arriving in China** — app stores in China may require Chinese payment methods for downloads.
- Set phone language to English **before** opening Alipay for the first time — affects the interface language.
- Use your home-country phone number for registration; the account is not tied to a Chinese SIM.
- Test with a small purchase at the airport upon arrival to confirm everything works.
- Alipay shows all payments in real-time — useful for tracking spending and verifying charges.
- **Long-stay option**: With a residence permit (3+ months), open a Bank of China account and link to Alipay for zero platform fees.
