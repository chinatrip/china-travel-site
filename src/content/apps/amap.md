# English navigation with Amap (高德地图)

## What can I use for English navigation?

Shanghai’s guide to Amap (高德地图) describes driving, public transport, walking and cycling routes in English. Save the destination’s name and address so you can check the selected location before leaving.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/7fbc0bbb51b341fcbb65c83986a6e32a.html)

## How can I explain a destination to a driver?

Canada’s travel advisory recommends carrying the destination and the name and address of your hotel written in Chinese characters. Ask your hotel for its Chinese address and keep a copy you can show without a connection.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china)

## Which places have English labels on Amap?

Amap’s English map provides English labels for places including attractions, restaurants and hotels. For a specific destination, keep the Chinese name or address from your booking so you can check that the result is the right place.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20250123/78d115a256af4c29a607b3d326ac2d8a.html)

## What trip details can I see in Amap before leaving?

Enter your start and destination, then open a transit route to see where to board the bus or metro, where to get off and the walking connection. In biking mode, Amap distinguishes bicycle and e-bike routes and shows estimated time and distance.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20240223/06d761174b294dad869d395cc3e38382.html)

## What should I save in Amap before landing?

You can save your hotel and the destinations you plan to visit before departure in Amap (高德地图). Keep the Chinese address and branch name handy for pickup or directions.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-EasyShanghai/20260713/379bcea6e1bd4defaa7db2451d68d3dd.html)

## Travel tip — Finding the right address

Search with the Chinese name if an English search returns several results. Compare the district and street number, then save the pin or a screenshot. A screenshot of the last few turns can help if the connection drops.

## Where can I download AMap Global (高德地图) on iPhone?

Look for AMap Global (高德地图) in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id461703208)

## Where can I find the Android app for AMap Global (高德地图)?

The linked Google Play listing is for AMap Global. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.autonavi.minimap)

## Can I use Amap offline?

Amap’s Chinese app help describes downloading offline city map data and searching the downloaded area. Check for the feature in your version, download the right city while connected and test a search in airplane mode. Live traffic and every feature of the international edition are outside that guide’s scope.

[wap.amap.com](https://wap.amap.com/offline/index.html)

## Travel tip — Meeting on the right street level in Chongqing

At Hongyadong (洪崖洞) in Chongqing, streets meet the building on both the first and the eleventh levels. When arranging a pickup, send the pin and the street or entrance name. A photo of the sign and a message such as 我在一楼入口 (I am at the first-floor entrance) can save a trip to the wrong level.

[Street levels at Hongyadong](https://cq.gov.cn/zwgk/zfxxgkml/zdlyxxgk/ggwh/ly/zxdt/202408/t20240826_13556747.html)

## Travel tip — Requesting a ride with Amap

If you already have Amap (高德地图), look for 高德打车 (Amap ride-hailing) when you need a ride. Check the pickup point, the available car types and the estimated fare for that trip before ordering. If you also checked another ride app, confirm that you have only one active request.

[Amap ride-hailing service](https://developer.amap.com/api/skill/ready-to-use/amap-ride-hailing-skill)
