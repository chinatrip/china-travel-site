# Reserving a hotel with Trip.com (携程)

## Can visitors reserve accommodation using Trip.com?

The government’s visitor guide lists Trip.com (携程) as an online hotel reservation option, alongside booking by telephone. If you plan to use an international bank card at the hotel, ask the hotel in advance whether it accepts your card.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/cmsres/11/11a3dd13306c44d6a744ce1575ad8ed4/016e480d2524ff7a6b905577d6557c77.pdf)

## Can a hotel refuse foreign guests because it lacks a special qualification?

A joint Chinese government notice instructs local authorities and online platforms not to impose qualification requirements that restrict hotels from receiving overseas guests. Hotels must still register guests. Confirm your booking and take your passport to check in.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2024/art_10ddb29858c24ece86b3f575bda1d9c4.html) · [www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## Who registers my accommodation?

Hotels register their foreign guests. For a stay outside a hotel, the foreign guest or host must register with the local public-security authority within 24 hours of moving in.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## How do I look for a pre-booked airport transfer?

The Ministry of Commerce guide describes the Airport Transfer option under Cars in Trip.com. Enter the flight details and check the quoted price and meeting instructions before confirming.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## Which services can I compare when booking my trip?

Shanghai lists Ctrip (携程) for flights, hotels and trains, and China Railway 12306 for buying train tickets directly. For a room in Shanghai you can also compare Meituan. Match dates, room type and cancellation conditions before comparing totals.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20231214/3cac2d8cc7f847da92a72252bae06705.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html)

## Travel tip — Before paying for a reservation

Compare the booking platform with the operator on the same dates. Check cancellation terms, baggage or breakfast where relevant, and the final total. Save the confirmation and any separate operator reference.

## Where can I ask Trip.com about a booking change?

Use Trip.com customer support for the booking. Check the cancellation terms for the particular room, ticket or transfer, including any supplier conditions. Save the booking reference before contacting support.

[www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/pages/support/?locale=en-xx) · [www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/contents/service-guideline/terms.html?curr=QAR&locale=en-US)

## Where can I download Trip.com on iPhone?

Look for Trip.com in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id681752345)

## Where can I find the Android app for Trip.com?

The linked Google Play listing is for Trip.com: Book Flights, Hotels. Check the listing and availability on your own device.

[play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ctrip.english)

## Is English-language Trip.com support open around the clock?

Trip.com lists 24-hour global English assistance for flights, hotels, trains and cars. Other booking types and local-language lines can have different hours. Open Customer Service in the app for the channel that covers your booking.

[www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/ask/questions/trip.com-call-center.html/)

## Can I book Chinese attraction tickets on Trip.com?

Trip.com lists attraction tickets among the products supplied through its website and app. Check the individual listing, accepted identification, cancellation policy and official venue channel. Inventory varies by venue.

[www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/contents/service-guideline/terms.html?curr=QAR&locale=en-US)

## Travel tip — Handling a disruption

If weather changes your plans, use the booking details to contact Trip.com or the operator. Ask for the change or refund terms in writing and keep the reply with your reservation.

## Does Trip.com charge a fee for China train tickets?

Trip.com’s Australian booking guide says a platform service fee can appear in the price breakdown before payment. Compare the same train, date and seat class on Trip.com and 12306, and check the final total for your booking.

[Trip.com](https://au.trip.com/guide/train/12306-vs-trip.com.html)
