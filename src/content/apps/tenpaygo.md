# TenPayGo for visitors

## What is TenPayGo?

Shenzhen reported the launch of TenPayGo on 24 September 2026. Its announcement describes linking an overseas bank card, a supported overseas wallet or Apple Pay to pay at merchants that accept WeChat Pay. Check support and fees for your own payment method.

[www.sz.gov.cn](https://www.sz.gov.cn/en_szgov/aboutsz/videos/content/post_13000672.html)

## Travel tip — Avoiding a duplicate charge

If the result is unclear, check the transaction history and ask the merchant before paying again. Keep the transaction reference for a refund or support enquiry. Never share a live payment code.

## How do I set up TenPayGo and check the payment method?

The Guide inside TenPayGo for iOS (version 1.0.4) describes signing in with email and adding an international card or enabling Apple Pay on a supported device. At a merchant, follow the prompts to scan their code or show yours. Check the merchant, amount, fee and payment method on screen before confirming. Open Guide in your installed version for its instructions.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenpaygo/id6778755338)

## Where can I check a TenPayGo refund or failed payment?

In the iOS app’s Guide (version 1.0.4), Transactions is where you check payment and refund status. It directs you to the merchant to initiate a refund and to support for unresolved issues. Timing depends on the merchant, card network and issuer. Check there before paying a second time.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenpaygo/id6778755338)

## Where can I download TenPayGo on iPhone?

Look for TenPayGo in the App Store. The US storefront listed the download at no charge on 20 November 2026. Service charges and purchases inside the app are separate. Check availability in the storefront used by your Apple account.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6778755338)

## Which cards can I use with TenPayGo?

Tencent’s US listing names Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay International, American Express, JCB, Discover and Diners Club. It also lists Apple Pay on iPhone. Check the method offered for your card and merchant before confirming.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenpaygo/id6778755338)

## What does TenPayGo charge in fees?

The iOS in-app Guide (v1.0.4) says service fees may apply and the final amount is shown before confirmation. It does not specify a flat rate. Read the amount and fee for the actual payment before you approve it.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenpaygo/id6778755338)

## Is TenPayGo publicly available yet?

Tencent publishes TenPayGo on the US iPhone App Store. Check whether your own storefront and device offer it. The listing supports availability there, not an identical global rollout.

[apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenpaygo/id6778755338)

## Travel tip — Choosing a transit code for Shenzhen

A merchant payment code and a transport gate code are different. If your wallet does not show a Shenzhen transit code, Shenzhen Tong lists 深圳通+ (Shenzhen Tong Plus), its WeChat mini program, as one way to open its passenger code. Check the setup before you reach the gate.

[Shenzhen Tong passenger QR codes](https://www.shenzhentong.com/ewmcp.html)
