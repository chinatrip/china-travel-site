# TenPayGo: Tencent's Payment App for Foreign Visitors to China (2026)

TenPayGo is a payment app from **Tencent**, built by the **WeChat Pay** team for foreign visitors to China. Its pitch: register with only an **email address**, add an international card, Apple Pay or a supported overseas e-wallet, and pay at merchants that already accept **WeChat Pay** — without the Chinese phone number or domestic bank account that have long been the friction points for tourists. After a limited test from late June 2026, Tencent **formally launched it on 24 September 2026** (source: Xinhua, 2026-09-24).

## What is TenPayGo?

TenPayGo is Tencent's standalone app for overseas visitors. Its payments run on Tencent's existing WeChat Pay network — the Google Play listing describes it as "payment capabilities powered by Weixin Pay" (checked 2026-10-08) — so you are paying the same merchant network as WeChat Pay, but through a much lighter onboarding:

- **Sign up with just an email address** — no Chinese mobile number and no domestic bank account required.
- **Link an international card** — Tencent says cards issued abroad by **seven major international card networks**, including Visa and Mastercard, are supported (source: Xinhua, 2026-09-24). TenPayGo's own in-app guide (July 2026) shows **Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club International and JCB**, and launch-day Chinese press names **UnionPay** among the seven (source: CLS via 21jingji, 2026-09-24).
- **Or pay with Apple Pay**, using a card already stored there.
- **Or link an overseas e-wallet** — added in September 2026. Tencent names **Starryblu** as one supported wallet (source: Xinhua, 2026-09-24). More wallets connected to WeChat Pay's cross-border QR gateway are being added gradually; Jiemian reports a target of about 60 by the end of 2026 (via sina.cn, 2026-09-24). Check the app for whether your home wallet is supported yet.
- **Pay** by scanning a merchant's QR code or showing your own pay code for the merchant to scan; the July 2026 in-app guide also lists checkout on supported merchant websites inside the app.
- Developer: **Tencent Technology (Shenzhen)**. Platforms: **iOS** (App Store, listed "Only for iPhone") and **Android** (Google Play, package `com.tencent.wxpai`). Interface: **English** (App Store listing, checked 2026-10-08).

## Is TenPayGo available yet?

**Yes — it has been officially launched.** How it got there:

- **Late June 2026:** the app quietly appeared on the App Store (v1.0 is dated 2026-06-21). Tencent described it as a "life-convenience assistant" for inbound visitors that was *still in small-scale testing and not fully open* (Tencent statement carried by Chinese media, 2026-06-28).
- **July–August 2026:** the signup gate opened to more users and the app updated roughly every one to two weeks (v1.0.4 on 2026-07-01 through v1.0.12 on 2026-08-29, per the App Store version history).
- **24 September 2026:** Tencent **formally launched** TenPayGo at an international payment services event in Shenzhen's Qianhai district, ahead of the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen in November (sources: Xinhua; CLS via 21jingji). Overseas e-wallet support had arrived in v1.1.0 on 2026-09-12, and v1.1.2 shipped on launch day (App Store version history; Google Play shows the same release notes, updated 2026-09-24).

**Signup and identity checks.** Registration is still by email after the launch. WeChat Pay's product lead told CLS that **verification at the payment step follows the applicable rules**, so you may be asked for further checks when adding a card or paying, even though signup itself is light (source: CLS via 21jingji, 2026-09-24). The app is still adding features, so expect onboarding and rules to keep changing.

## What else can TenPayGo do?

Beyond payments, Tencent is positioning TenPayGo as a travel helper. These are announced or reported features — check the app for what is live for you:

- **Shenzhen bus and metro ride code.** Shenzhen Tong says its ride code is being built into TenPayGo, so visitors can ride Shenzhen buses and the metro with no phone number, real-name check or transit card. Full opening was expected in **mid-October 2026** (source: Shenzhen Special Zone Daily, 2026-09-29). It covers Shenzhen only.
- **Announced next:** departure tax refunds and AI-powered menu translation (Tencent via Xinhua/China.org.cn, 2026-09-25). Launch coverage also reports in-app object identification, where you circle an item on screen to identify it (source: Lowyat.NET, 2026-09-25).

## Do you actually need TenPayGo?

For most visitors, **it is a convenience, not a necessity.** Since the **April 2024 PBOC policy**, foreigners can already bind a foreign Visa or Mastercard **directly to Alipay or WeChat Pay** — no Chinese bank account or phone number — and reach essentially the same merchants (see [[wiki/concepts/mobile-payment.md]]). TenPayGo does not unlock a payment network you otherwise couldn't reach.

What TenPayGo *does* offer is a **lighter setup**: email-only signup instead of the fuller Alipay/WeChat registration and passport flow. It is most interesting if you want minimal onboarding, if your home e-wallet is supported, or if you will be in Shenzhen and want the ride code. If you already have Alipay or WeChat Pay working, there is little reason to switch.

## How do you set up and pay with TenPayGo?

Based on TenPayGo's in-app guide (captured July 2026, app v1.0.4) plus launch reporting. The app has been updated several times since, so screens may differ:

1. **Download TenPayGo** from the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android).
2. **Sign up with an email address.**
3. **Add a payment method:** an international bank card, Apple Pay, or a supported overseas e-wallet. You may be asked to verify your card or Apple Pay setup for security.
4. **Pay** by scanning the merchant's QR code or showing your own pay code for the cashier to scan. Review the merchant, amount, and any fees before you confirm; payment settles over the WeChat Pay network. **Safety tip from the app: never screenshot, save, or share your pay code.**

## What does TenPayGo cost, and what are the limits?

**TenPayGo has not published its own fee schedule or spending caps.** The in-app guide sets the ground rules; Tenpay's rule for WeChat and user reports fill in the specifics:

- **What the app itself says:** a payment with an international card or Apple Pay **may include a service fee**, and the fee and final amount are **always shown before you confirm** — but no amounts are published (source: in-app guide, July 2026, Part Five).
- **The WeChat benchmark.** Tenpay, which runs WeChat Pay, publishes a rule for international cards in the WeChat app: **free up to ¥200 per transaction, 3% of the full amount above that** (source: Tenpay notice, 2026-01-15). That notice covers WeChat and does not name TenPayGo.
- **What users report.** Many TenPayGo users report that same pattern — no fee up to ¥200, about 3% above. Others report **3% on every amount**. So the only reliable figure is the one on your own payment screen.
- **Limits.** The app states limits **may apply per transaction, per day, or per year**, and that some payment methods become unavailable once a limit is reached — but the numbers are not published (source: in-app guide, Part Five).
- **Refunds.** Refunds are initiated by the merchant and usually returned to the original card; arrival time depends on the merchant, card network, and issuing bank (source: in-app guide, Part Six).
- **Practical read:** if the fee-free range up to ¥200 applies to your account, small everyday payments are the sweet spot — a coffee, a metro ride, a shared bike. A 3% cut makes it less attractive for large purchases, where the same economics on Alipay/WeChat, or a foreign-card POS swipe, apply anyway.
