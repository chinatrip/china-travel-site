# Individual tax residence in China

## How does Chinese law define an individual tax resident?

Article 1 distinguishes domicile from days present. A person domiciled in China meets the resident definition. For a person without domicile in China, the threshold is at least 183 cumulative days in China in the tax year. Nationality alone does not decide domicile or the tax due.

[12366.chinatax.gov.cn](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-1.html)

## Which taxes use the 3%–45% rate schedule?

Article 3 applies progressive rates of 3%–45% to comprehensive income and 5%–35% to business income. These are marginal rate schedules. Do not apply the highest rate to all income.

[12366.chinatax.gov.cn](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-1.html)

## How does the statutory deduction work?

Article 6 provides a CNY 60,000 annual basic deduction for a resident individual’s comprehensive income, plus applicable deductions. For a nonresident’s wages and salaries it provides a CNY 5,000 monthly deduction. Other categories of income have different calculations.

[12366.chinatax.gov.cn](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-1.html)

## When is annual reconciliation due?

Under Article 11, a resident who is required to complete the annual reconciliation for comprehensive income does so between 1 March and 30 June of the following year. Check whether you have a filing obligation for the year in question.

[12366.chinatax.gov.cn](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/082/082-5-1.html)

## Can a foreign tax resident combine housing allowances and special additional deductions?

Through 31 December 2027, a qualifying foreign tax resident can choose the special additional deductions or the exemptions for eligible allowances such as housing, language training and children’s education under the rules cited in Notice 29. The two treatments cannot be combined or switched within the same tax year.

[www.gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900701.htm)

## When may a resident skip annual comprehensive-income reconciliation?

For income received in 2024–2027, a resident may skip reconciliation if annual comprehensive income is at most CNY 120,000 and extra tax is due, or if the additional tax due is at most CNY 400. This exemption does not apply if the withholding agent failed to withhold and prepay as legally required.

[www.gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm)

## How does the six-year rule work for someone without a domicile in China?

For a non-domiciled individual resident in China for at least 183 days this tax year, look back at the six preceding years, counting from 2019 at the earliest. If any of those years had fewer than 183 days of residence, or any single departure in those years exceeded 30 days, the exemption covers income both sourced outside China and paid by an overseas entity or individual.

[www.chinatax.gov.cn](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5248304/content.html)

## What are the annual marginal tax bands for a resident’s comprehensive income?

Apply the rates to annual taxable comprehensive income after applicable deductions. The first CNY 36,000 is taxed at 3%. Successive portions up to CNY 144,000, 300,000, 420,000, 660,000 and 960,000 are taxed at 10%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 35% respectively. The portion above CNY 960,000 is taxed at 45%.

[12366.chinatax.gov.cn](https://12366.chinatax.gov.cn/bzds/079/079.html)

## Where can I file annual individual income tax reconciliation?

The Ministry of Commerce guide lists local tax service halls, the Individual Income Tax app and the Natural Person E-Tax portal. Its instructions for foreign nationals using the app or portal for the first time direct them to a local tax service hall for a registration code. Nonresident individuals do not file the resident annual comprehensive-income reconciliation.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/AssetsZi/A_Guide_to_Working_and_Living_in_China_as_Business_Expatriates_2025.pdf)

## How do I claim an income-tax treaty benefit?

Check that the treaty and benefit apply to your circumstances. Eligible individuals may claim when filing themselves or through the withholding agent and should retain supporting documents for later inspection. Consult the State Taxation Administration’s treaty text for your jurisdiction.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/AssetsZi/A_Guide_to_Working_and_Living_in_China_as_Business_Expatriates_2025.pdf)

## What family expenses can qualify for special additional deductions?

Eligible care for a child under three and eligible children’s education each have a CNY 2,000 monthly standard per child. For eligible elderly support, an only child may deduct CNY 3,000 a month. Siblings share CNY 3,000, with a CNY 1,500 monthly maximum per sibling. Other categories include continuing education, serious illness medical expenses, housing loan interest or housing rent. Each has its own conditions.

[app.www.gov.cn](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202308/31/506746/article.html) · [tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn](https://tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn/11200000000/0300/030005/20260506101227073.shtml)

## Is salary withholding calculated the same way for tax residents and non-residents?

For a tax resident’s wages, the withholding agent uses the cumulative withholding method and files monthly. For a non-resident’s wages, it withholds monthly under the non-resident rules. Other types of income have their own calculation and withholding rules.

[www.chinatax.gov.cn](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html) · [tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn](https://tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn/11200000000/0600/060003/06000321/20260313112250583.shtml)

## What if my withholding agent did not withhold individual income tax?

Article 13 of the Individual Income Tax Law requires the taxpayer to pay by 30 June of the following year when the withholding agent did not withhold tax on taxable income. If the tax office specifies a payment deadline, pay by that deadline.

[www.chinatax.gov.cn](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html)
