# India 印度 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## Where does the government guide direct applicants?
The government guide directs fresh and reissue applications to the Ministry of External Affairs Passport Seva portal: register, complete the appropriate application, then pay and schedule an appointment.

Scope: Basic application route described by Kheda district government; not a complete national eligibility or evidence checklist. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [District Kheda, Government of Gujarat](https://kheda.nic.in/service/create-passport/)

## What do Normal and Tatkal options establish?
The guide separates fresh and reissue applications and lists Normal and Tatkal options. The existence of an option does not establish an individual applicant’s eligibility; obtain the applicable document checklist from the responsible passport office.

Scope: Basic application route described by Kheda district government; not a complete national eligibility or evidence checklist. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [District Kheda, Government of Gujarat](https://kheda.nic.in/service/create-passport/)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
