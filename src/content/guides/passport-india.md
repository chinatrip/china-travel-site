# India Passport: Application Preparation

## Where does the government guide direct applicants?

The government guide directs fresh and reissue applications to the Ministry of External Affairs Passport Seva portal: register, complete the appropriate application, then pay and schedule an appointment.

Scope: Basic application route described by Kheda district government; not a complete national eligibility or evidence checklist

[kheda.nic.in](https://kheda.nic.in/service/create-passport/)

## What do Normal and Tatkal options establish?

The guide separates fresh and reissue applications and lists Normal and Tatkal options. The existence of an option does not establish an individual applicant’s eligibility; obtain the applicable document checklist from the responsible passport office.

Scope: Basic application route described by Kheda district government; not a complete national eligibility or evidence checklist

[kheda.nic.in](https://kheda.nic.in/service/create-passport/)

## What does the Chinese embassy in India list for a tourist L visa?

For a tourist L visa in India, prepare your itinerary, hotel and round-trip flight bookings. For financial evidence, the embassy accepts either six months of personal savings statements consistently showing an average monthly balance of at least INR 100,000, or a personal deposit certificate for a comparable amount deposited at least three months before applying. The bank must sign and stamp the certificate. If a parent’s or guardian’s statement is used in special circumstances, include the account holder’s signed authorization. If you have an inviter in China, see the L-visa table for the invitation and the inviter’s identification.

Scope: Chinese embassy tourist visa checklist for applications in India.

[in.china-embassy.gov.cn](https://in.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/qz/202408/t20240820_11476941.htm) · [in.china-embassy.gov.cn](https://in.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/qz/202408/W020261022030118880819.jpg)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

India’s embassy in Beijing asks Indian citizens working, studying or visiting Beijing to register with it. This consular registration is separate from accommodation registration with Chinese authorities.

Scope: Advice for travellers from India.

[www.eoibeijing.gov.in](https://www.eoibeijing.gov.in/register.php)
