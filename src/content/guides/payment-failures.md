# Foreign card payment failures in Alipay and WeChat Pay

## What if Alipay or WeChat Pay cannot verify my foreign card?

Linking an overseas card requires verification by the card issuer. If verification fails, contact the issuer or the wallet’s customer service. Supported cards, limits and fees depend on the wallet. Check the information displayed for your own transaction.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## What does it cost to use a linked overseas card?

For an eligible overseas card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, the Shanghai government guide says the platform fee is generally waived for a transaction of CNY 200 or less. Above CNY 200 a 3% fee may apply to the entire purchase. Check the amount shown at checkout and any separate fees from your issuer.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)

## Where can I obtain RMB cash?

The Ministry of Commerce lists commercial banks, authorized money changers and self-service exchange machines. Look for currency-exchange signage. An ATM that accepts your international card is another option.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## Travel tip — When a checkout fails

Read the error message and ask whether the merchant has another payment method. If a charge appears pending, check the wallet or card record before retrying. Keep the receipt or order reference for support, and hide payment codes and personal details in screenshots.

## Why can I pay a shop but not send my friend money from the same card?

Shanghai’s guide distinguishes merchant payments from transfers and red packets (红包). A linked foreign credit card can be used for eligible merchant payments, but the guide excludes transfers and red packets funded by that card. This does not establish that every small merchant uses a personal code.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-BankingServices/20231215/fe3a293d065f40e0a7f740aa88edb050.html)

## Travel tip — Checking a merchant code and account limit

Ask the cashier which merchant code to use or whether another payment method is available. Check the limit shown in your own wallet before a large purchase, and ask your bank about card and foreign transaction fees.

## Travel tip — What to tell the bank or wallet support

Keep the error wording and the time, amount and merchant name from the failed attempt. Check whether the transaction is pending before trying another card. Ask the provider about that transaction while you can still identify the purchase.

## Travel tip — A card terminal or restaurant code does not work

If a shop’s card terminal fails, ask the cashier which payment methods that checkout accepts. Check your bank or wallet history before retrying, and keep the receipt or order number if the shop and the app show different results. A useful question is 可以换一种付款方式吗？ (May I pay another way?).
