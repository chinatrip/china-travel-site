# Germany 德国 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## How do I submit a passport photo?
Apply through the responsible municipality/passport authority. Since 1 May 2025, the cited guidance requires digital photographs transmitted through the authorized process: a photographer uploads to a secure service and supplies a retrieval code, or the authority may take the photo on site.

Scope: German passport applications; this government service entry is configured for Bavaria. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [德国联邦政务门户；内容发布方 Bayern](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99085001012000/herausgeber/BY-358/region/09)

## Does this guidance apply throughout Germany?
The selected region is Bavaria. Confirm local appointment availability, fees and document requirements with the responsible office; do not treat this regional page as the complete procedure for every German municipality.

Scope: German passport applications; this government service entry is configured for Bavaria. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [德国联邦政务门户；内容发布方 Bayern](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99085001012000/herausgeber/BY-358/region/09)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
