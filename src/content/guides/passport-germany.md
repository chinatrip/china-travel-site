# Germany Passport: Application Preparation

## How do I submit a passport photo?

Apply through the responsible municipality/passport authority. Since 1 May 2025, the cited guidance requires digital photographs transmitted through the authorized process: a photographer uploads to a secure service and supplies a retrieval code, or the authority may take the photo on site.

Scope: German passport applications; this government service entry is configured for Bavaria

[verwaltung.bund.de](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99085001012000/herausgeber/BY-358/region/09)

## Does this guidance apply throughout Germany?

The selected region is Bavaria. Confirm local appointment availability, fees and document requirements with the responsible office; do not treat this regional page as the complete procedure for every German municipality.

Scope: German passport applications; this government service entry is configured for Bavaria

[verwaltung.bund.de](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99085001012000/herausgeber/BY-358/region/09)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

Germany’s foreign ministry recommends enrolling in its crisis preparedness register before visiting China.

Scope: Advice for travellers from Germany.

[www.auswaertiges-amt.de](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/laender/china-node/chinasicherheit-200466)
