# Registering accommodation in China

## Who registers my accommodation?

Hotels register their foreign guests. For a stay outside a hotel, the foreign guest or host must register with the local public-security authority within 24 hours of moving in.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## How do I register outside a hotel?

Since 21 September 2026 the guest or host can register nationwide through the NIA service website, the NIA 12367 app, or NIA mini programs in WeChat or Alipay. In-person registration remains available.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## Do I need to register again after a short trip?

NIA identifies limited exceptions after registration, including returning to a home in China owned by you or your spouse, and returning to the same habitual residence within the validity of the same residence document or permanent residence card. Check which condition applies before relying on it.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c237273/content.html)

<span id="if-you-miss-the-24-hour-deadline"></span>

## What happens if private accommodation registration is missed?

Article 76 provides for a warning and allows an additional fine of up to CNY 2,000 for failure to register under Article 39(2). Contact the local public-security authority to complete the registration.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html) · [en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c237273/content.html)

## How can I confirm my online accommodation registration?

Completed online registration has the same legal effect as on-site registration. After submitting through the NIA service, check Registration Record Inquiry for the record. The NIA advises getting the host’s help with the initial registration, as they know the address details.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c237273/content.html)

## What photos does Beijing’s accommodation registration guide request?

Beijing lists a photo of the passport or travel-document information page, a bareheaded photo taken in real time and a photo of the address page of the property ownership certificate or lease agreement. Use the instructions for the specific local or national channel where you apply.

[english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/livinginbeijing/applicationfordocuments/202107/t20210719_2439278.html)

## What counts as a habitual residence when checking whether I must register again?

The National Immigration Administration uses habitual residence for a home where you live or plan to live long term, meaning more than 180 days. For the return-to-the-same-habitual-residence exception, you must already have registered and return to that address while the same residence permit or permanent resident card remains valid. The length of a stay alone does not qualify you.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c237273/content.html)

## Can I print my Shanghai accommodation registration after filing it online?

Shanghai’s accommodation registration rules say that once a self-service submission is approved, you can download the registration form (境外人员住宿登记表) and print it if you need a paper copy.

[www.shanghai.gov.cn](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/grzsdjsjzc/20240823/e4dd3d2df80448f59145f133fec0dd71.html)

## Travel tip — Staying with a host

Save the host’s address and contact details. Agree who will handle registration and keep the confirmation where you can find it after a move.

## Travel tip — Finding the registration again

Keep the registration slip or online confirmation and a copy of any later update. If the portal fails, ask the responsible office how to complete the process and which documents to bring.
