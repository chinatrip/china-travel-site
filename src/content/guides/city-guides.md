# China city itineraries by season and length of stay

## Travel tip — Northwest Yunnan in 10–11 days

Try Kunming (昆明) for 2 days, Dali (大理) for 3, Lijiang (丽江) for 3 and Shangri-La (香格里拉) for 2–3. Consider April if you want to fit the lower stops into the dry season. Yunnan’s provincial overview puts the wet season in May–October and notes the much colder climate of the northwest. In summer, give a lakeside or mountain day a movable date. In colder weather, spend longer in Dali if conditions on the final leg do not suit you. These are planning days, including transfers.

[Yunnan provincial climate overview](https://www.ynxc.gov.cn/html/2024/yns_0418/11831.html)

## Travel tip — Beijing to Shanghai via Xi’an and Chengdu in 12–16 days

For history and food, try Beijing (北京) for 4–5 days, Xi’an (西安) for 2–3, Chengdu (成都) for 3–4 and Shanghai (上海) for 3–4. Late autumn is an option if Beijing’s red leaves are a priority. In summer, put outdoor walks early in the day and keep a museum or indoor food stop for the afternoon. With only a week, leave Chengdu for another trip.

[Beijing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism, autumn walks](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/travellinginbeijing/citytours/202411/t20241106_3935132.html)

## Travel tip — Shanghai and the Jiangnan gardens in 8–10 days

For a spring garden trip, start with Shanghai (上海) for 3–4 days, Hangzhou (杭州) for 2, Suzhou (苏州) for 1 and Nanjing (南京) for 2–3. Try April for this version of the trip; pick actual garden dates after checking local flower updates. If a lakeside walk is rained out, swap it with a museum visit. For a slower pace, sleep in Suzhou and shorten the Shanghai leg.

## Travel tip — Southwest food and scenery in 11–12 days

Try Chengdu (成都) for 3 days, Chongqing (重庆) for 2, Zhangjiajie (张家界) for 3 and Guilin/Yangshuo (桂林／阳朔) for 3–4. If the mountain walks matter most, consider April or October, two months highlighted by Hunan tourism for Zhangjiajie. Check holiday dates and leave one outdoor day flexible for fog or rain. With less time, pick the mountains or the river towns.

[Hunan tourism department, Zhangjiajie seasons](https://whhlyt.hunan.gov.cn/whhlyt/wldhlylx/202208/t20220816_27584141.html)

## Travel tip — Xi’an to Kashgar in about 8 days

For a Silk Road city trip, try Xi’an (西安) for 3 days, Ürümqi (乌鲁木齐) for 2 and Kashgar (喀什) for 3. Consider October if the autumn poplars near Kashgar are a priority. Check the local foliage update for the year, flight times between stops and the rules for any border-area side trip. Allow another day if connections take most of a sightseeing day.

[Kashgar autumn tourism guide](https://www.kashi.gov.cn/ksdqxzgs/c106707/202509/5c37778dbdb44fe591e3de87d2929600.shtml)

## Travel tip — Choosing an itinerary by interest

For an initial list, pair Beijing and Xi’an for historical sights, or Shanghai with Suzhou or Hangzhou for a city-and-gardens trip. Choose fewer stops if you want time to wander. Check transport and entry or transit conditions for the actual route before paying.

## Travel tip — Sample routes for seven or fourteen days

For a seven-day history and city trip, try three days in Beijing (北京), two in Xi’an (西安) and two in Shanghai (上海), allowing for transfers. For two weeks, one option is Beijing on days 1–4, Xi’an on days 5–6, Zhangjiajie (张家界) on days 7–11 and Shanghai on days 12–14. Drop a stop if you prefer fewer moves. Check entry permission for every stop before booking.

## Travel tip — Building a route around food, history or hiking

For a food-focused holiday, compare Chengdu (成都), Chongqing (重庆) and Guangzhou (广州). If historic towns interest you, consider Datong (大同), Pingyao (平遥) and Xi’an (西安). For a landscape-focused trip, compare Guilin (桂林), Yangshuo (阳朔) and Zhangjiajie (张家界). Look at door-to-door travel times before combining them.

## Travel tip — Fenghuang and Mutianyu on a September itinerary

On their September 2025 trip, the RealChinaGuide authors visited Fenghuang Ancient Town (凤凰古城) from Zhangjiajie. They liked walking along the river, but would stay overnight or spend that day in Zhangjiajie next time. Their hotel-arranged driver cost about CNY 400 and took about 3½ hours each way on that trip. In Beijing, they would move the Mutianyu Great Wall (慕田峪长城) earlier in their stay, when their legs were fresher.

## Travel tip — Kashgar’s Sunday livestock market and the Karakoram Highway

For the Sunday livestock market in Kashgar (喀什), search specifically for 牛羊大巴扎 (livestock bazaar). Ask your host to confirm the market day and map pin before leaving. For a separate mountain trip, the G314 heads southwest from Kashgar towards the Pamirs along the Karakoram Highway (喀喇昆仑公路). Give your operator your passport nationality, dates and planned stops so they can check road conditions and any required documents before you pay. A visit to the market and a mountain road trip require different amounts of time.
