# Travel apps for a trip to China

## What can I use for English navigation?

Shanghai’s guide to Amap (高德地图) describes driving, public transport, walking and cycling routes in English. Save the destination’s name and address so you can check the selected location before leaving.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/7fbc0bbb51b341fcbb65c83986a6e32a.html)

## Can I book a train with my passport?

The 12306 English website and app accept passports for train bookings. If online verification needs manual review, upload the passport information page as directed or take the valid passport to a station counter. Bring the original document used to book when you travel.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## How can I request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce lists Alipay, WeChat and DiDi as channels for ordering a ride. Set the pick-up point and destination and check the vehicle and fare shown for your trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## What does it cost to use a linked overseas card?

For an eligible overseas card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, the Shanghai government guide says the platform fee is generally waived for a transaction of CNY 200 or less. Above CNY 200 a 3% fee may apply to the entire purchase. Check the amount shown at checkout and any separate fees from your issuer.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)
