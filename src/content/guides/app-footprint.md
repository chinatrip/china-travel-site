# Travel apps for a trip to China

## What can I use for English navigation?

Shanghai’s guide to Amap (高德地图) describes driving, public transport, walking and cycling routes in English. Save the destination’s name and address so you can check the selected location before leaving.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/7fbc0bbb51b341fcbb65c83986a6e32a.html)

## Can I book a train with my passport?

The 12306 English website and app accept passports for train bookings. If online verification needs manual review, upload the passport information page as directed or take the valid passport to a station counter. Bring the original document used to book when you travel.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## How can I request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce lists Alipay, WeChat and DiDi as channels for ordering a ride. Set the pick-up point and destination and check the vehicle and fare shown for your trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## What does it cost to use a linked overseas card?

For an eligible overseas card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, the Shanghai government guide says the platform fee is generally waived for a transaction of CNY 200 or less. Above CNY 200 a 3% fee may apply to the entire purchase. Check the amount shown at checkout and any separate fees from your issuer.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)

## Which apps should I install for a first trip to China?

Pick the apps that fit your plans. For meals, Meituan (美团) offers delivery and Dianping (大众点评) helps you find restaurants and read reviews. Use Amap (高德地图) for directions, DiDi (滴滴出行) for a ride and Railway 12306 (铁路12306) for trains. WeChat (微信) is useful for messaging. Alipay (支付宝) and WeChat Pay (微信支付) are options for paying merchants.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20231214/3cac2d8cc7f847da92a72252bae06705.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/cmsres/46/46fb01aff21a41d48bd4ae57be8bbda9/3e350b415280adf5ba42fe7ee7ca866e.pdf)

## Travel tip — Setting up only what you need

Choose a payment method, a map and a way to contact your host first. Add a ride, train or delivery app if it fits the itinerary. Open each app while you can receive a verification text and try a destination search before relying on it.

## Can I use these China services in a web browser?

China Railway offers web ticketing on 12306.cn. Trip.com also takes reservations on its website. Other tasks may use an app or mini program: for example, Shanghai lists a WeChat mini program for Meituan attractions. Check the operator for your particular booking.

[www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html) · [www.trip.com](https://www.trip.com/contents/service-guideline/terms.html?curr=QAR&locale=en-US) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html)

## Which apps must I install as a separate download?

Choose by task. You can buy train tickets on the 12306 website and use some Shanghai ticketing services inside WeChat or Alipay. If you prefer a standalone map, Amap is one option. No fixed minimum number of apps follows from these service guides.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-UsefulApps/20231214/3cac2d8cc7f847da92a72252bae06705.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## Travel tip — Using Apple Maps as a second map on iPhone

On an iPhone, try Apple Maps for a second view of the route. Apple identifies Amap (高德地图) as the mapping service behind its China product. Before leaving your hotel, search for the destination in Chinese and save the entrance or a screenshot. Compare the walking route with the street signs after you arrive.

[Apple Maps and privacy](https://www.apple.com/hk/en/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/)
