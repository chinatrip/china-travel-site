# China Visa and Visa-Free Entry: Check Your Route

Your entry route depends on your passport, travel purpose, dates and itinerary. A visa exemption is not a guarantee of admission. Start with this comparison, then verify your exact route with the responsible Chinese embassy or NIA before buying non-refundable tickets.

## Which entry route applies to my trip?

| Route | Main condition | Stay / area |
|---|---|---|
| Unilateral waiver | Ordinary passport on the 50-country list; eligible purpose | Up to 30 days; check policy expiry |
| Bilateral exemption | Your passport and purpose meet the relevant agreement | Agreement-specific; not automatically 30 days |
| 240-hour transit | Eligible nationality, designated entry port and confirmed onward travel to a third country or region | 240 hours within permitted areas |
| Hainan regional waiver | Ordinary passport on the separate 61-country list | Up to 30 days within Hainan (海南) |
| 24-hour transit | Valid international travel document and confirmed onward seat on a flight, ship or train to a third country or region | Within the port's restricted area; leaving requires temporary entry approval |
| Tourist L visa | Tourism when an exemption does not cover the trip | Follow the visa's entries, validity and duration of stay |

These are separate schemes: eligibility for one does not imply eligibility for another. The sections below link their governing sources.

<span id="section-a-unilateral-30-day-visa-free-entry"></span>

## Who can use the unilateral 30-day waiver?

The [NIA nationality list](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183390/content.html) contains **50 countries**, including Japan, Germany, France, the UK and Canada. The United States and Singapore are not on this unilateral list; Singapore has a separate bilateral agreement.

| Region | Ordinary-passport nationalities |
|---|---|
| Europe (35) | Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom |
| Oceania (2) | Australia, New Zealand |
| Asia (7) | Bahrain, Brunei, Japan, Kuwait, Oman, South Korea, Saudi Arabia |
| Americas (6) | Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Peru, Uruguay |

The [Chinese Embassy FAQ dated 19 August 2026](https://ch.china-embassy.gov.cn/ls_fw_s/sbqz/202608/t20260819_12006793.htm) gives these conditions:

- Tourism, business, family/friend visits, exchanges and transit qualify; work, study and journalism do not.
- Use an ordinary passport valid for the intended stay. Temporary/emergency travel documents do not qualify under this waiver.
- Brunei has no stated expiry, Russia is covered through **31 December 2027**, and the other 48 countries through **31 December 2026** in that notice. On 10 November the [Slovak foreign ministry](https://www.mzv.sk/en/pressreleasedetail?articleId=60664557&groupId=10182&p_p_id=sk_mzv_portal_pressrelease_detail_portlet_PressReleaseDetailPortlet&p_p_lifecycle=0) reported that China was prepared to extend the waiver for Slovak citizens. For travel in 2027, check the [published Chinese terms](https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/lh/lhqz_149493/cjwd/) before booking.
- The 30-day clock starts the day after entry. Bring evidence matching your purpose, such as accommodation and onward travel details.
- A planned longer stay requires an appropriate visa. An unexpected need to stay longer requires an application to local exit-entry authorities; approval is not automatic.

<span id="section-b-bilateral-mutual-visa-exemption"></span>

## How is Singapore's bilateral exemption different?

Under the [Singapore embassy guidance](https://sg.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfwx/va/zgqz/202609/t20260918_12025190.htm), ordinary Singapore passports qualify for visits of up to 30 days under the bilateral agreement effective 9 February 2024. Activities requiring prior approval, including employment or journalism, and stays exceeding 30 days require the appropriate visa. For another nationality, consult its own agreement rather than applying Singapore's terms.

<span id="section-c-240-hour-transit-visa-free"></span>

## Who qualifies for 240-hour transit?

The [NIA’s Chinese-language transit policy of 20 August 2026](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741577/c1731205/content.html) lists **57 nationalities**, **65 designated entry ports** and permitted areas in **24 provincial-level regions**. An ordinary passport and confirmed onward ticket with a departure date and seat to a third country or region are required. A return to the same country/region immediately before China does not meet that transit pattern. Check the complete nationality and port tables linked here; a country's presence on the unilateral list is not sufficient.

Albania, Argentina, Australia, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Brazil, Brunei, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Indonesia, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Kyrgyzstan, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, New Zealand, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Republic of Korea, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Singapore, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States, Vietnam.

Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam appear in the NIA’s Chinese transit list. Liechtenstein appears in the separate [unilateral visa-exemption list](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183390/content.html).

The [NIA conditions annex](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741542/c1751080/content.html) requires at least **three months** remaining passport/travel-document validity. You must also meet the [entry requirements of your onward destination](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1751080/content.html). The annex’s older nationality count must not replace the newer 57-country roster. Work, study and journalism still require prior approval and the appropriate visa.

## Can I travel anywhere within those 24 regions?

No. The [consular permitted-area table (September 2026)](https://toronto.china-consulate.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/HQGR/50/202407/t20240709_11451153.htm) lists six regions with local limits. The other 18 are Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Liaoning, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Fujian, Shandong, Henan, Hubei, Hunan, Guangdong, Chongqing, Shaanxi, Anhui, Hainan and Guizhou. Within the six restricted regions, use this table:

| Region | Permitted cities / prefectures |
|---|---|
| Shanxi (山西) | Taiyuan, Datong |
| Heilongjiang (黑龙江) | Harbin only |
| Jiangxi (江西) | Nanchang, Jingdezhen |
| Guangxi (广西) | Nanning, Liuzhou, Guilin, Wuzhou, Beihai, Fangchenggang, Qinzhou, Guigang, Yulin, Hezhou, Hechi, Laibin |
| Sichuan (四川) | Chengdu, Zigong, Luzhou, Deyang, Suining, Neijiang, Leshan, Yibin, Ya’an, Meishan, Ziyang |
| Yunnan (云南) | Kunming, Yuxi, Chuxiong, Honghe, Wenshan, Pu’er, Xishuangbanna, Dali, Lijiang |

**Aba / Jiuzhaigou is outside Sichuan's listed area.** 阿坝州 (Aba Prefecture), including 九寨沟 (Jiuzhaigou), must not be offered as an eligible stop under this transit route. You may cross provincial borders while staying within designated areas. Check connecting travel through other regions with border inspection. Check every stop and exit port, not just the arrival airport. See [[wiki/concepts/china-transit-area-check.md]]. [Aba Prefecture government](https://www.abazhou.gov.cn/abazhou/c102028/abgk.shtml).

## When does the 240-hour clock end?

It starts at 00:00 on the day after entry. For entry on Monday **16 November 2026**, the clock starts Tuesday **17 November at 00:00**; adding 240 hours reaches **Friday of the following week, 27 November at 00:00**, China time. Plan departure before that boundary and follow the deadline actually granted by border inspection. This arithmetic does not guarantee a permit. Source: [NIA transit conditions](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741542/c1751080/content.html).

<span id="section-e-24-hour-airside-transit"></span>

## Can I leave the airport during 24-hour transit?

The rule covers international aircraft, ships and trains through open ports. Staying within the restricted port area for no more than 24 hours is distinct from entering the city. To leave that area, first obtain temporary entry permission from the port's border-inspection authority. Approval is not guaranteed. Source: [NIA 24-hour transit policy](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html).

At some airports, eligible passengers connecting directly on international flights can stay in the restricted area and skip border inspection. Check your transfer with the airline or the port. [NIA notice on direct transfers](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1751080/content.html).

## Does Hainan's 30-day waiver cover mainland travel beyond Hainan?

This regional route covers ordinary passports from **61 countries**, including the United States, for stays within 海南 (Hainan) of up to 30 days. It permits tourism, business, visits, medical treatment, conferences and sports, but not work or study. It does not itself authorize travel beyond Hainan; another applicable entry basis or authorization would be needed. Source: [NIA regional visa-free policies](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c180637/content.html).

## How do I apply for a tourist visa or extend a stay?

For tourism requiring a visa, apply for an **L visa** through the Chinese embassy/consulate responsible for where you apply and its designated service channel. Do not assume a worldwide application checklist, price, processing time or grant length.

For example, the [US application instructions](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202509/t20250920_11712385.htm) require more than six months of passport validity and at least two blank visa pages. Routine L applicants within the United States no longer submit flight/hotel bookings, itineraries or invitation letters under the cited simplification; individual cases can require further documents. Those are application-location rules, not universal rules for all US citizens abroad.

For a visa authorizing a stay of up to 180 days, Article 29 requires an applicant who needs an extension to apply to the local exit-entry administration seven days before the authorized stay expires. An extension is not guaranteed. Visa validity, number of entries and permitted stay length are different fields. Source: [Exit and Entry Administration Law, Articles 29 and 37](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html).

## What should I prepare before arrival?

Use the official [[wiki/concepts/china-arrival-card.md|arrival-card guide]]; online completion is an option, not a universal 72-hour deadline. See [[wiki/concepts/accommodation.md]] and [NIA accommodation registration notice](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html).

For a private stay, the nationwide options include the NIA service website, the NIA 12367 app and its WeChat or Alipay mini programs. You can also register in person with local public security. ## What does Hainan’s own visa exemption cover?

Ordinary passport holders from the 61 countries on the National Immigration Administration’s Hainan list, including the United States, can enter through Hainan’s open ports and stay within the province for up to 30 days. ## Can border inspection refuse entry if my planned activity conflicts with my visa type?

Yes. Article 25 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law requires refusal of entry if a visitor may engage in activities incompatible with their visa type. ## Travel tip — Checking the itinerary before buying tickets
