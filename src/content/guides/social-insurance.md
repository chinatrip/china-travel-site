# Social insurance for foreign employees in China

## Which social insurance schemes are listed for foreign employees?

For foreigners legally hired by an employer in China, the measures cover basic pension, basic medical, work injury, unemployment and maternity insurance. Where a social security agreement applies, follow that agreement. Confirm coverage for your country and employment situation.

[www.beijing.gov.cn](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/202509/t20250908_4194428.html)

## What happens to my personal social insurance account if I leave China before pension age?

You may keep the account. If you return to work in China, contribution periods are added together. You may instead request termination in writing and receive the personal account balance as a lump sum. On death, the remaining individual account balance can be inherited.

[rsj.beijing.gov.cn](https://rsj.beijing.gov.cn/english/policies/socialinsurance/202507/t20250721_4153933.html)

## When must my employer register me for social insurance?

For a foreign employee covered by these measures, the employer must register the employee for social insurance within 30 days of the date the employment permit is obtained.

[rsj.beijing.gov.cn](https://rsj.beijing.gov.cn/english/policies/socialinsurance/202507/t20250721_4153933.html)

## Does the China–Kyrgyzstan agreement waive all social insurance?

The bilateral agreement took effect on 14 October 2025. In China it provides basic pension exemptions for covered groups, including qualifying posted workers from Kyrgyzstan. Check the category and documentation with the social insurance authority. Nationality alone does not exempt someone from all five schemes.

[chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn](https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/c/2025-10-15/455155.shtml)

## Who pays the employee social insurance contributions?

Under the Social Insurance Law, employers and employees contribute to basic pension, basic medical and unemployment insurance. The employer pays work-injury and maternity contributions. An applicable bilateral agreement can affect coverage for eligible foreign workers.

[www.samr.gov.cn](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_e81d115419b4463ebb59ec46467fb136.html)

## Which medical insurance rates does Shanghai publish for employees?

Shanghai’s notice applies from 1 March 2026 through 28 February 2027. It sets the employer’s basic medical contribution, including maternity, at 8.5%, plus a local medical supplement of 0.5%. The employee contribution is 2%. The notice covers medical contributions for this period.

[www.shanghai.gov.cn](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/gwk/search/content/921e047144694b61b6df8ca0c5ef2cfc)

## How do I claim a social-insurance treaty exemption in Shanghai?

Overseas insurance alone does not exempt you from contributions in China. If an agreement in force covers your case, your employer in Shanghai applies in person to the social insurance agency where you are registered, with documents such as a certificate of coverage. Which contributions are exempt, and for how long, depends on the agreement.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-FAQS-WorkinShanghai/20260828/dd1042c8b283404d84c74ea6012d63e0.html)

## Can I stop paying contributions because China has signed a social-security agreement with my country?

Check whether the agreement is in force, covers your posting and contributions, and requires a certificate. For example, the Chinese ministry reported that Austria and China signed an agreement on 6 November 2026, following agreements signed with 13 other countries. The announcement of signature alone does not establish an exemption for your payroll.
