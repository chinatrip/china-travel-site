# Singapore Passport: Application Preparation

## How do I submit an application?

The ICA application uses Singpass and a digital colour passport photograph taken within the last three months. Overseas residents can also submit the overseas form through a Singapore mission.

Scope: Singapore citizens; distinguish local and overseas applications

[www.ica.gov.sg](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/identity-card-and-passport/singapore-passport/singapore-passport-application)

## What are the published fees and processing estimates?

The standard application fee is S$70 online and S$80 over the counter. ICA estimates around 1–2 weeks locally and 4–6 weeks overseas for complete applications; these are estimates, not promised delivery dates. For a newborn Singapore citizen’s first passport, ICA waives the application fee when the application is submitted via MyICA on or before the child’s first birthday. [ICA](https://www.ica.gov.sg/documents/singapore-passport)

Scope: Singapore citizens; distinguish local and overseas applications

[www.ica.gov.sg](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/identity-card-and-passport/singapore-passport/singapore-passport-application)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

Singapore’s foreign ministry encourages travellers to eRegister their trip so its missions can contact them if needed.

Scope: Advice for travellers from Singapore.

[www.mfa.gov.sg](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/travelling-overseas/travel-advisories-notices-and-visa-information/china/)
