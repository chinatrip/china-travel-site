# Singapore 新加坡 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## How do I submit an application?
The ICA application uses Singpass and a digital colour passport photograph taken within the last three months. Overseas residents can also submit the overseas form through a Singapore mission.

Scope: Singapore citizens; distinguish local and overseas applications. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [新加坡移民与关卡局 ICA](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/identity-card-and-passport/singapore-passport/singapore-passport-application)

## What are the published fees and processing estimates?
The published fee is S$70 online and S$80 over the counter. ICA estimates around 1–2 weeks locally and 4–6 weeks overseas for complete applications; these are estimates, not promised delivery dates.

Scope: Singapore citizens; distinguish local and overseas applications. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [新加坡移民与关卡局 ICA](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/identity-card-and-passport/singapore-passport/singapore-passport-application)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
