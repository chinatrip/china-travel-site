# China arrival card: online, paper and exemptions

Official arrival information preparation, distinct from a visa or admission approval.

## Must I complete the arrival card before boarding?
Since 20 November 2025, eligible foreign travelers may submit arrival information before travel through NIA services. If unable to do so beforehand, on-arrival QR, terminal or paper completion remains available. The cited notice does not set a universal 72-hour-before-boarding requirement.

Scope: Foreign travelers required to complete an arrival card. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻美国大使馆发布，原发布机关国家移民管理局](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202512/t20251203_11765346.htm)

## Who is exempt from completing the arrival card?
The notice exempts permanent-resident-card holders, non-Chinese Hong Kong/Macao mainland-permit holders, group-visa/group-waiver travelers, 24-hour direct-transit passengers remaining within the designated port area, same-vessel cruise return passengers, e-channel users and foreign transport crew.

Scope: Only the seven categories specified in the notice. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻美国大使馆发布，原发布机关国家移民管理局](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202512/t20251203_11765346.htm)

## Coverage limits
Use the official NIA arrival-card channel. Completing a card does not confer visa-free eligibility or guarantee entry. Do not apply one exemption to all transit passengers.
