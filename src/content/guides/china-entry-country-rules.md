# China Entry Rules by Passport and Application Location

Country-specific rules for Japan, Singapore, United States, Germany, France, UK, Australia, Canada, South Korea and India must not be inferred from language or departure airport.

## Which sampled nationalities qualify for the unilateral waiver?
The cited Chinese embassy FAQ lists Japan, Germany, France, the UK, Australia, Canada and South Korea among eligible ordinary-passport nationalities for visits up to 30 days for the specified business, tourism, family/friends, exchange or transit purposes. Work, study and journalism are outside this waiver.

Scope: Named ordinary-passport holders; mainland China; qualifying purposes. Checked: 2026-11-17. Recorded policy end: 2026-12-31; do not assume continuation beyond this date.
Sources: [中国驻瑞士大使馆](https://ch.china-embassy.gov.cn/ls_fw_s/sbqz/202608/t20260819_12006793.htm)

## What passport validity applies to this waiver?
For this unilateral waiver, the ordinary passport must cover the intended stay; non-ordinary temporary/emergency documents do not qualify under this rule. Do not substitute the different passport-validity rules for visa applications or 240-hour transit.

Scope: Unilateral visa waiver only. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻瑞士大使馆](https://ch.china-embassy.gov.cn/ls_fw_s/sbqz/202608/t20260819_12006793.htm)

## What rules apply to Singapore ordinary passports?
The China–Singapore ordinary-passport agreement permits stays not exceeding 30 days without a visa. Activities needing prior approval, such as employment or journalism, and intended stays over 30 days require the appropriate visa in advance.

Scope: Singapore ordinary passport; mainland China bilateral agreement. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻新加坡大使馆](https://sg.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfwx/va/zgqz/202609/t20260918_12025190.htm)

## Do US visa applicants need flight and hotel bookings?
For tourist L-visa applications submitted in the United States, the Chinese mission notice removes the routine flight-booking, hotel-booking, itinerary and invitation-letter submissions; case-specific requests remain possible. Follow the consular district and COVA instructions. This is an application-location rule, not a universal exemption for US citizens applying elsewhere.

Scope: Tourist L applications within the United States. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻美国大使馆](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202509/t20250920_11712385.htm)

## Coverage limits
The US and India are not in the cited 50-country unilateral list; that does not rule out every other entry route. Transit needs its own nationality, route, port and area checks. Recheck policies for entry after the recorded end date.
