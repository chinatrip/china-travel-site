# China private accommodation registration online

Official preparation for staying with friends, family or in a private residence.

## Who registers my accommodation and by when?
Hotels handle foreign guest registration. For accommodation outside hotels, the foreign guest or host must register with the local public-security authority within 24 hours of moving in.

Scope: Foreign guests; distinguish hotel and non-hotel accommodation. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [国家移民管理局](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## Can I complete private accommodation registration online?
From 21 September 2026 the national rollout allows the guest or host to use the NIA service website, the NIA 12367 app, or its WeChat/Alipay mini programs. The in-person route remains available and online registration has equal effect. Follow the service workflow for the applicant and host.

Scope: Nationwide non-hotel registration; channel availability does not remove the deadline. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [国家移民管理局](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## Coverage limits
This is accommodation registration, not a residence permit or extension of the allowed stay. A booking receipt alone is not proof that registration has been completed.
