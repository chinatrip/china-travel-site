# United States 美国 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## How do I apply for a first adult passport?
Use Form DS-11 with citizenship evidence, identification, a photo and applicable fees. Submit at an authorized acceptance facility and sign as instructed by the acceptance agent. Renewal eligibility is a separate process.

Scope: First-time adult US passport applicants in the United States; renewal and minors have separate rules. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [美国国务院 Passport Services](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/adults.html)

## How much processing time should I allow?
Allow both processing and mailing time. The published routine processing estimate is 4–6 weeks, excluding mailing; check the agency again before committing to travel.

Scope: First-time adult US passport applicants in the United States; renewal and minors have separate rules. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [美国国务院 Passport Services](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/adults.html)

## Can I fly to China with a US passport card?
A US passport card cannot be used for international air travel; travel to China by air requires the appropriate passport book, not the card alone.

Scope: US passport card versus passport book. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [美国国务院 Passport Services](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/passport-card-vs-book.html)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
