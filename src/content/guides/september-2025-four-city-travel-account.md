# Four cities in September 2025: a China travel diary

Trip by **Agus and Lu (RealChinaGuide)**. Account retold by **ChinaGuide** from their September 2025 travel diary.

## Travel tip — How Agus and Lu divided a September trip among four cities

In 北京 (Beijing), their first walk started near 前门 (Qianmen). The demanding day for them was the one they spent exploring the imperial sights on foot. Looking back, they would visit 慕田峪长城 (the Mutianyu Great Wall) while their legs were still fresh.

In 西安 (Xi’an), they had time for the 西安城墙 (Xi’an City Wall) as well as the 兵马俑 (Terracotta Warriors). Rain changed an evening plan. For travelers drawing up a similar visit, those two stops make better anchors than a long list of evening sights.

## Travel tip — What they would change in Zhangjiajie and Shanghai

Their base for 张家界 (Zhangjiajie) was 武陵源 (Wulingyuan). Fog limited the views on 天门山 (Tianmen Mountain). They also enjoyed 凤凰古城 (Fenghuang Ancient Town), but the drive there and back left too little time in town. Their preference for a repeat trip was to spend a night or skip that detour. Keeping a day free would leave room to move the mountain visit if the forecast changes.

In 上海 (Shanghai), one of their evenings paired 南京路 (Nanjing Road) with 外滩 (the Bund). They were happy to see the skyline from the riverfront and passed on going up 上海中心大厦 (Shanghai Tower).
