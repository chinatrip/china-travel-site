# Hotels and Accommodation Registration in China

Book a property that confirms your check-in arrangements, payment method and cancellation terms. A special “foreign guest licence” should not be presented as a legal requirement for accepting foreign visitors.

## Do hotels need a special licence for foreign guests?

The [July 2024 seven-department accommodation notice](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2024/art_10ddb29858c24ece86b3f575bda1d9c4.html) says authorities and platforms should not use qualification requirements to restrict accommodation providers from receiving overseas guests, and providers/platforms should not unlawfully publish blanket refusals. It also calls for registration training, language support and improved payments. These policy directions do not guarantee that every front desk will handle every booking smoothly.

Before booking, ask the property to confirm the document you will use, arrival time and how it handles registration. Save its answer and Chinese address. If refused on a supposed qualification ground, ask the manager or booking platform for help; the notice identifies **12367** as a channel for policy enquiries and complaints. Do not assume an automatic refund or replacement room.

## Who must register my accommodation?

Hotels must register foreign guests and report the information to local public security authorities. If staying **outside a hotel**, you or your host must complete registration within **24 hours after arrival** at the accommodation.

Since **21 September 2026**, the NIA notice provides national online handling through its government service website, the **移民局12367 (NIA 12367)** app and WeChat/Alipay mini programs. In-person registration remains available and has equal validity. A platform booking alone is not proof of completed registration. Source: [NIA national accommodation registration notice](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html).

For a private home or rental, agree beforehand who will submit the registration and retain the completion record. If the online process fails, contact local public security or 12367 promptly instead of waiting past the deadline. See [[wiki/concepts/china-accommodation-registration-online.md]].

## What should I confirm before check-in?

This is a practical checklist, not a universal list of legal documents:

- Carry the original valid identity/travel document used for your reservation, and ask the property about any additional documents it needs.
- Confirm the name on the booking, late-arrival arrangement and Chinese address.
- Ask for the total room price, taxes/charges, cancellation deadline, deposit amount and release timing in writing.
- Confirm the actual card or wallet you intend to use, including whether it can cover the deposit. A platform's payment options do not establish the hotel's options.
- Ask the front desk to confirm registration was completed. Keep the booking and payment records.

## Should I choose a hotel, hostel or private rental?

Compare the specific property's arrangements rather than relying on a star rating or chain name. Check reception hours, language assistance, accessibility, room/bathroom type, location and cancellation terms. For a private rental, resolve the non-hotel registration responsibility with the host before paying.

Compare the final price and refund terms across platforms and the property itself. This guide does not guarantee identical inventory, foreign-card acceptance, English staffing or a particular discount on any platform. See [[wiki/entities/trip-com.md]] for platform context and [[wiki/concepts/mobile-payment.md]] for payment preparation.

## Where can I compare hotel listings before booking in Shanghai?

Shanghai’s accommodation guide lists Ctrip (携程) and Meituan (美团) for comparing and booking hotels. Ctrip lets you filter by location, price, star rating and amenities. Compare the same room, dates and cancellation terms when checking prices.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html)

## What happens to a hotel deposit in Shanghai?

Under Article 15 of Shanghai’s hotel rules, a hotel collecting a deposit agrees the amount with the guest at booking or check-in and gives a receipt. If the deposit exceeds what you owe, the hotel must return the balance at settlement.

[www.shanghai.gov.cn](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/xxzfgzwj/20210608/e1ceccf2959645d8b2b85ad0dae3ea95.html)

## Travel tip — Comparing a hostel, hotel or apartment

Decide whether you need a private bathroom, lift, laundry, quiet room or late check-in. Compare the same dates and room, and check the final total, cancellation deadline and breakfast terms. For a private stay, agree with the host how you will register.

## Travel tip — Keeping the booking handy

Save the property’s Chinese name, address, phone number and confirmation offline. Ask the hotel about payment, deposit and late arrival before booking if these affect your choice.

## Travel tip — Asking for help before check-in

If you need English-language assistance or a particular payment method, contact the property or booking support in advance. Ask whether breakfast is included for your rate and keep the written reply.

## Can I use Airbnb or short-term rentals in China?

Airbnb reported removing its mainland China listings in July 2022 when it closed its domestic business. If you arrange a private stay through another channel, confirm the address and who will handle accommodation registration. The historical announcement does not establish the availability of a particular property.

[news.airbnb.com](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-q4-2022-and-full-year-financial-results) · [www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## Travel tip — Room prices from one September trip

RealChinaGuide reported these room costs for two travellers in September 2025: about €50 a night in Beijing and Shanghai, €40–45 in Xi’an and €35–40 in Zhangjiajie. Use the figures to see how one trip varied by stop. For your dates, compare a hostel bed per person, a private room per night and a hotel near your first morning’s departure. If meeting people is important, look at hostel common areas; if you arrive late, check reception hours.

## Travel tip — Request a non-smoking room

If smoke bothers you, ask before booking: 请问可以预订无烟房吗？ (Can I reserve a non-smoking room?) Ask the property to confirm the room type in writing.

## Travel tip — Find a homestay or a hostel

For a homestay, try Tujia (途家民宿) in its app or scan the WeChat mini program code on its website. Meituan Minsu (美团民宿) also has a dedicated homestay app. Before paying, ask the host how passport check-in and accommodation registration will work for that address. For hostels, Hostelworld has Beijing listings with dorm beds and private rooms. Enter your dates and party size, and compare the total for everyone staying. Recent comments about noise, bathrooms or late check-in can be more informative than a score alone.

[Tujia booking options](https://www.tujia.com/) · [Meituan Minsu service channels](https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/71) · [Hostelworld Beijing listings](https://www.hostelworld.com/hostels/asia/china/beijing/)

## Travel tip — Compare a dorm bed with a private room

In a RealChinaGuide account archived in June 2026, the traveller recalled paying about CNY 70 per night for a dorm bed on stays in Xi’an and Chengdu, and about CNY 350 per night for hotel accommodation on stays in Beijing and Shanghai. The account gives no stay dates. For two travellers sharing a room, compare the cost of two dorm beds with the total nightly room price for the same dates.

## Travel tip — Four city budgets from the June 2026 guide

For a starting point when drafting a budget, ChinaGuide’s June 2026 guide used these illustrative nightly amounts in yuan for budget and mid-range hotels respectively: Beijing ¥180–280 and ¥400–700; Shanghai ¥200–300 and ¥450–750; Chengdu ¥150–250 and ¥350–600; Xi’an ¥130–220 and ¥300–550. The old estimates have no named property, check-in dates or booking conditions. Replace them with quotes for your dates, room occupancy and cancellation terms before deciding what you can afford.

## Travel tip — Another place to compare hotels and a deposit question for check-in

飞猪 (Fliggy) is another booking option. For example, Shanghai Disney Resort lists its official Fliggy store among its hotel booking channels. Compare the same room, dates and cancellation terms with the property. For a 客栈 (guesthouse) or hotel, you can ask 入住需要押金吗？ (is a deposit required at check-in?) and 押金怎么退？ (how will the deposit be returned?). Confirm the amount, payment method and release time for your own booking.

[Shanghai Disney Resort’s hotel and ticket booking channels](https://www.shanghaidisneyresort.com/en/ticket/)

## Travel tip — A dated DiDi Hotel observation in the English app

ChinaGuide’s July 2026 in-app note recorded a 滴滴酒店 (DiDi Hotel) option on the English home screen. That version showed Latin-letter guest names, an international phone field and a passport ID reminder. Treat this as an observation from one version. If the tile appears in your app, inspect the booking provider, property, final payment screen and cancellation terms before paying. If it is missing, contact the hotel or use another booking channel.
