# Hotels and Accommodation Registration in China

Book a property that confirms your check-in arrangements, payment method and cancellation terms. A special “foreign guest licence” should not be presented as a legal requirement for accepting foreign visitors.

## Do hotels need a special licence for foreign guests?

The [July 2024 seven-department accommodation notice](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2024/art_10ddb29858c24ece86b3f575bda1d9c4.html) says authorities and platforms should not use qualification requirements to restrict accommodation providers from receiving overseas guests, and providers/platforms should not unlawfully publish blanket refusals. It also calls for registration training, language support and improved payments. These policy directions do not guarantee that every front desk will handle every booking smoothly.

Before booking, ask the property to confirm the document you will use, arrival time and how it handles registration. Save its answer and Chinese address. If refused on a supposed qualification ground, ask the manager or booking platform for help; the notice identifies **12367** as a channel for policy enquiries and complaints. Do not assume an automatic refund or replacement room.

## Who must register my accommodation?

Hotels must register foreign guests and report the information to local public security authorities. If staying **outside a hotel**, you or your host must complete registration within **24 hours after arrival** at the accommodation.

Since **21 September 2026**, the NIA notice provides national online handling through its government service website, the **移民局12367 (NIA 12367)** app and WeChat/Alipay mini programs. In-person registration remains available and has equal validity. A platform booking alone is not proof of completed registration. Source: [NIA national accommodation registration notice](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html).

For a private home or rental, agree beforehand who will submit the registration and retain the completion record. If the online process fails, contact local public security or 12367 promptly instead of waiting past the deadline. See [[wiki/concepts/china-accommodation-registration-online.md]].

## What should I confirm before check-in?

This is a practical checklist, not a universal list of legal documents:

- Carry the original valid identity/travel document used for your reservation, and ask the property about any additional documents it needs.
- Confirm the name on the booking, late-arrival arrangement and Chinese address.
- Ask for the total room price, taxes/charges, cancellation deadline, deposit amount and release timing in writing.
- Confirm the actual card or wallet you intend to use, including whether it can cover the deposit. A platform's payment options do not establish the hotel's options.
- Ask the front desk to confirm registration was completed. Keep the booking and payment records.

## Should I choose a hotel, hostel or private rental?

Compare the specific property's arrangements rather than relying on a star rating or chain name. Check reception hours, language assistance, accessibility, room/bathroom type, location and cancellation terms. For a private rental, resolve the non-hotel registration responsibility with the host before paying.

Compare the final price and refund terms across platforms and the property itself. This guide does not guarantee identical inventory, foreign-card acceptance, English staffing or a particular discount on any platform. See [[wiki/entities/trip-com.md]] for platform context and [[wiki/concepts/mobile-payment.md]] for payment preparation.

## Where can I compare hotel listings before booking in Shanghai?

Shanghai’s accommodation guide lists Ctrip (携程) and Meituan (美团) for comparing and booking hotels. Ctrip lets you filter by location, price, star rating and amenities. Compare the same room, dates and cancellation terms when checking prices.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Tourism-Accommodation/20260813/aa459a1a8c904a61b49381717e308188.html)

## What happens to a hotel deposit in Shanghai?

Under Article 15 of Shanghai’s hotel rules, a hotel collecting a deposit agrees the amount with the guest at booking or check-in and gives a receipt. If the deposit exceeds what you owe, the hotel must return the balance at settlement.

[www.shanghai.gov.cn](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/xxzfgzwj/20210608/e1ceccf2959645d8b2b85ad0dae3ea95.html)
