# SIM cards and eSIM service in China

## Where can I get a local SIM card?

Bring your passport or Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card to a service centre of a telecom operator such as China Mobile, China Telecom or China Unicom. Ask about data, voice minutes and the documents needed for the plan you want.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/quickguideservices/purchasingsimcards/)

## Will a particular eSIM work on my phone?

The Ministry of Commerce says some operators offer eSIM service. Before buying, give the operator your exact handset model, purchase region and travel dates and ask which plan it can activate. Verify roaming and access to any service you depend on with your provider.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/quickguideservices/purchasingsimcards/)

## How do I check the balance on a mainland Chinese SIM?

For China Mobile, text YE to 10086. For China Unicom, text 102 to 10010. For China Telecom, text 102 to 10001. If you prefer a voice call, the respective service numbers are 10086, 10010 and 10000.

[english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/quickguideservices/purchasingsimcards/simtips/202312/t20231219_3504017.html)

## How can I top up a mainland SIM card?

Beijing’s guide lists the operator’s service hall, official app and customer-service line. For a WeChat or Alipay mobile top-up, choose the mobile recharge option, check the number and pay with a linked bank card.

[english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/quickguideservices/purchasingsimcards/simtips/202312/t20231219_3504017.html)

## Can I order a SIM online or will I need to visit a shop?

The method depends on the carrier and city. Shanghai’s guide describes showing your passport at an operator’s store. Its Nihao Mobile example allows online ordering and asks for passport and entry documentation plus a video selfie to activate the SIM. Beijing separately lists online reservation with collection at Capital or Daxing airport. Check the instructions for the offer you choose.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Mobilephone/20260813/cb62ccf29651428f995a92255cd3291a.html) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/quickguideservices/purchasingsimcards/stepsim/202312/t20231219_3503994.html)

## Travel tip — Choosing data, calls and SMS

Ask whether the exact plan includes a local phone number, incoming calls, SMS and hotspot use. Compare it with roaming on your existing plan, including how you will receive bank verification messages. Save the operator’s activation and support details offline.

## Travel tip — Checking activation and top-ups

Check the provider’s activation trigger and expiry before installing a travel eSIM. Select the intended SIM for mobile data and test it while you can still contact support. Ask about top-ups and any speed limits before buying an unlimited plan.

## Travel tip — Matching the plan to your data use

Estimate how often you will stream video, upload photos or tether a laptop. Compare the plan’s speed and top-up terms, check your balance and keep your provider’s support contact.

## Do I need a VPN if I use a foreign eSIM in China?

It depends on the package and the connection. Airalo says its China, regional Asia and global plans with mainland China coverage route mobile data abroad and can reach Google and WhatsApp without a separate VPN. Its description applies while using that eSIM’s cellular data. Check your own provider and plan if you switch networks.

[Airalo](https://www.airalo.com/help/using-managing-esims/ZSEEHBT5HW6F/stay-connected-in-china-your-guide-to-unfiltered-internet-access-is-a-vpn-required/ZDZBNIKI4TFP)

## Travel tip — Comparing two plans for your own trip

Compare the same travel dates, data allowance, hotspot terms and whether each plan can receive the calls or texts you need. Date your price comparison and save the exact plan name; a similar-looking plan may have different conditions.

## Travel tip — Checking an airport SIM promotion

A July 2024 Shanghai report described a first-day 3 GB offer and separately priced short-term SIM cards at China Mobile’s Pudong Airport Terminal 2 counter. Treat that as a dated example. For your own trip, ask the operator which visitor packages are available, how long the allowance lasts, whether a phone number is included and what happens when the allowance runs out. The Tianjin Communications Administration directs visitors to operator-run counters with their passport and to the operator’s published tariffs.

[Shanghai report on the Pudong Airport offer](https://touch.shio.gov.cn/jsp/zfxw_detail_mobile.jsp?id=20240710225811831) · [Tianjin Communications Administration SIM guide](https://tjca.miit.gov.cn/bsfw/bszn/art/2026/art_4cdc69ee883e409990033d44b49e0fab.html)

## Travel tip — Comparing Nomad and Holafly with other China eSIMs

Nomad and Holafly both list China travel eSIMs. Compare their live plan pages with Ubigi or a local mobile operator for your dates. Check device compatibility, when the plan starts, days and data included, hotspot limits, phone number or SMS support, and what happens after a fair-use threshold. Save a copy of installation and support details before leaving Wi-Fi.

[Nomad China plans](https://www.nomadesim.com/china-eSIM) · [Holafly China plans](https://esim.holafly.com/esim-china/)
