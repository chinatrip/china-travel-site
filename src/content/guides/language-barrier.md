# Communicating a destination and finding an interpreter

## How can I explain a destination to a driver?

Canada’s travel advisory recommends carrying the destination and the name and address of your hotel written in Chinese characters. Ask your hotel for its Chinese address and keep a copy you can show without a connection.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china)

## Where can I look for help with interpretation?

The British government’s China travel advice links to information on translators and interpreters. For an appointment, ask the office which language assistance or documents it will accept.

[www.gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/china/getting-help)

## How can I explain a dietary preference?

You can say 我吃素 (I am vegetarian) or 我不吃肉 (I do not eat meat). For an allergy, name the specific ingredient and ask the restaurant to check how the dish is prepared. A preference alone does not convey an allergy.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-LearnChinese/20240408/233e22b629f64bf1bfe06d155a3109c7.html)

## How do I translate a message or menu in WeChat?

Press and hold a chat message, then choose Translate. For printed text such as a menu, tap +, choose Scan, select Translate and photograph the text.
