# South Korean passport application and travel preparation

## What should an adult prepare for a first South Korean passport?

For the domestic first-passport route for adults aged 18 or over, the Korean foreign ministry lists an application form, identification and one passport photograph taken within six months. A family-relations record certificate is also required unless the authority can verify it through the government information-sharing system. Generally apply in person. Check any military-service documents and requirements specific to your situation.

Scope: First ordinary passport for an adult applying domestically in South Korea.

[www.passport.go.kr](https://www.passport.go.kr/home/kor/contents.do?menuPos=3)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

In China, foreign nationals aged 16 or over must carry one of the following for police inspection: their passport, another international travel document, or their foreigner’s stay or residence document.

Scope: Advice for travellers from South Korea.
