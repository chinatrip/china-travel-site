# Using mini programs during a visit to China

## How do I register outside a hotel?

Since 21 September 2026 the guest or host can register nationwide through the NIA service website, the NIA 12367 app, or NIA mini programs in WeChat or Alipay. In-person registration remains available.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1806111/content.html)

## How can I request a ride?

The Ministry of Commerce lists Alipay, WeChat and DiDi as channels for ordering a ride. Set the pick-up point and destination and check the vehicle and fare shown for your trip.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## How can I buy a cinema ticket in Shanghai?

In WeChat, open Event Tickets under Payment and Services to reach Maoyan (猫眼). Choose a cinema, screening and seats. Alipay’s Movies entry opens Taopiaopiao (淘票票). Shanghai’s guide also lists Maoyan Pro in English for checking schedules and ratings. Use the regular ticketing service to buy a ticket.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-MobileandInternet-Popularapps/20260826/d4a59c454a294219a8a05369e341bb88.html)

## Which Shanghai visitor services are in Alipay’s Easy Go?

In Shanghai, search for Easy Go in Alipay (支付宝). The city lists food delivery, local transport, attraction information and ticket booking among its services. Check the operator, service area and final booking details in the mini program.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-ExpatServices-Videos-WhatsNew/20250702/104ac119548743968fe343bb14d14bdd.html)

## Travel tip — Finding a service again

Save the Chinese name of the service and verify the operator, city and branch when opening a mini program from a search or QR code. Keep a separate record of bookings and check the payment result before trying another checkout.

## How do I find an exhibition or show without installing another app?

In WeChat, search for a mini program by its Chinese name. Shanghai lists 猫眼 (Maoyan), 大麦 (Damai), 西岸生活 (Life West Bund) and 美团景点游玩 (Meituan attractions) as ticketing options. Check the venue’s own listing for the event you want.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html)

## What are WeChat and Alipay mini-programs?

These are services you can open inside the host app. For example, search WeChat for 猫眼 (Maoyan) to find shows, or open Easy Go in Alipay for Shanghai visitor services, including transport and attraction booking. The service you choose sets its own booking requirements.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-TravelTips/20241217/91a38689417640cd823efb33c2f4f8db.html) · [english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-ExpatServices-Videos-WhatsNew/20250702/104ac119548743968fe343bb14d14bdd.html)

## Travel tip — Looking up a nearby business

Search for the business by its Chinese name. If you prefer not to share your location, try entering the address or district manually. Before scanning a table code or paying, check the branch name with the staff.

## Travel tip — Saving a mini program you will use again

After opening a useful mini program in WeChat, check its menu for 添加到我的小程序 (add to My Mini Programs), if available in your version. Try reopening it before leaving the counter. Saving its Chinese name is an easy fallback if the menu looks different.
