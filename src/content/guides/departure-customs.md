# Customs allowances when returning from China

## What can I bring back to Canada after 48 hours?

Eligible returning travellers who have been away for at least 48 hours can claim a personal exemption of up to CAD 800 and must bring the goods with them for that exemption. After at least seven days some goods may follow separately. Alcohol and tobacco must accompany the traveller and have additional limits.

[travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/returning/customs/personal-exemptions)

## What if I exceed a goods allowance when returning to Great Britain?

When entering England, Scotland or Wales from outside the UK, exceeding a category’s allowance can mean tax and duty on the whole category. Check the separate rules for Northern Ireland.

[www.gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/bringing-goods-into-uk-personal-use/arriving-in-Great-Britain)

## Can I deduct the EU allowance from the price of one expensive item?

For eligible non-commercial goods brought by air or sea from outside the EU, the general allowance is EUR 430. The value of a single item cannot be split to fit the allowance. Check your country of entry for the duty and tax due.

[eur-lex.europa.eu](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32007L0074)

## Can I take a cultural relic out of mainland China?

For an item classified as a cultural relic, Article 78 requires review by a designated authority. Get an assessment before travelling with a possible cultural relic.

## What tobacco and alcohol allowances apply when entering mainland China?

Separate rules apply to frequent crossings, e-cigarettes and special customs areas. Under-18s do not receive these allowances. Declare goods above the applicable limits.

## Which customs channel should I use if I am unsure what to declare?

At a Chinese entry or exit point with red and green customs channels, take the red channel if you have goods that require a written declaration, are unsure of the rules, or do not know which channel applies. This is Article 12 of China Customs’ baggage rules.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_2412b6f8868b4034bf55a1261158e544.html)

## Which document do I present at China’s exit inspection?

Under Article 27 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law, foreign travellers present their passport or other international travel document to the border inspection authority and complete the required departure formalities.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n794014/n1050181/n1050479/c757592/content.html)

## May I bring plain tea leaves home to the United States?

USDA allows any quantity of tea made solely from Camellia sinensis leaves, including green, black and oolong tea. Declare it on entry. Blends with fruit, seeds or other plants are subject to different rules.

[www.aphis.usda.gov](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/traveling-with-ag-products/coffee-tea-honey-nuts-spices)

## How early should I get to the airport for an international departure from China?

A 2021 civil aviation white paper used 2½–3 hours as a typical planning allowance for international flights. For Changzhou Benniu, the airport recommended arriving two hours before international departures in its July 2026 notice. Check the instructions for your own airport, terminal and airline. The check-in cutoff is the latest time to complete check-in.

## Travel tip — Preparing the return journey

Keep the receipts for valuable purchases. Check your destination’s import rules for the actual items and amounts you will carry. Save the booking and leave time for any inspection before boarding.

## Travel tip — Packing purchases, batteries and paperwork

Make a list of batteries, food, alcohol and higher-value purchases before packing. Check airline, airport and destination customs instructions for each item. Save the receipts and check flight status on departure day.
