# Mobile payments with Alipay and WeChat Pay

## What does it cost to use a linked overseas card?

For an eligible overseas card linked to Alipay or WeChat Pay, the Shanghai government guide says the platform fee is generally waived for a transaction of CNY 200 or less. Above CNY 200 a 3% fee may apply to the entire purchase. Check the amount shown at checkout and any separate fees from your issuer.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-BankingandPayments-Payments/20260921/c2c94d06a0454c8fa05386b80d0ae865.html)

## How do I make a payment at a merchant?

The Ministry of Commerce guide describes two ways to pay by mobile wallet. Scan the merchant’s collection code, or show the payment code in your own wallet for the merchant to scan. Check the merchant and the amount before authorizing payment.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## What if Alipay or WeChat Pay cannot verify my foreign card?

Linking an overseas card requires verification by the card issuer. If verification fails, contact the issuer or the wallet’s customer service. Supported cards, limits and fees depend on the wallet. Check the information displayed for your own transaction.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html) · [www.12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html)

## Which overseas wallet is HiPay, and which is GCash?

China’s 2025 guide lists HiPay under Mongolia and GCash under the Philippines among participating overseas wallets. It also lists Kakao Pay under South Korea and Touch’n Go eWallet under Malaysia.

[tr.china-embassy.gov.cn](https://tr.china-embassy.gov.cn/tur/sgxw/202508/P020250812770217257636.pdf)

## How can I fund the UnionPay Nihao China app?

Shanghai reports that UnionPay launched Nihao China in December 2025 for overseas visitors. Its guide lists Visa and Mastercard for online top-ups. This describes adding funds to the app.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-EditorsPick-ShopinShanghai/20260819/05e547ffeabe4e1c8f5c927ba6e8f762.html)

## Can I pay cash when a business takes payments in person?

China’s cash-payment rules require businesses that can collect payment face to face to accept RMB cash when collecting in person, serving customers face to face, or fulfilling an online order in person. Unattended services must explain how a customer can pay with cash if mobile payment is unavailable.

[www.hunan.gov.cn](https://www.hunan.gov.cn/zqt/zcsd/202512/t20251222_33876116.html)
