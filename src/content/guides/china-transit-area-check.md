# 240-hour transit: Sichuan Jiuzhaigou and restricted travel areas

Official area restrictions and deadline calculation for transit itineraries.

## Can I visit Jiuzhaigou on 240-hour transit?
Sichuan is limited to Chengdu, Zigong, Luzhou, Deyang, Suining, Neijiang, Leshan, Yibin, Yaan, Meishan and Ziyang. Aba (including Jiuzhaigou) is not on that list. Heilongjiang is limited to Harbin. Guangxi and Yunnan also use specified city/prefecture lists, not whole-region access.

Scope: 240-hour transit; official September 2026 table. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻多伦多总领事馆](https://toronto.china-consulate.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/HQGR/50/202407/t20240709_11451153.htm)

## How are the passport conditions and deadline calculated?
The passport/travel document must have at least three months remaining, and a confirmed onward journey to a third country or region is required. The clock starts at 00:00 on the day after entry. For example, entry on Monday 2026-11-16 gives a clock start of 2026-11-17 00:00 and +240 hours is Friday 2026-11-27 00:00, China time; follow the actual permit deadline.

Scope: Eligible nationality, approved port and itinerary; not a guaranteed grant. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [国家移民管理局](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741542/c1751080/content.html)

## Can I leave the airport during a 24-hour transit?
Under 24-hour transit, a traveler needing to leave the designated port area may apply to the port border-inspection authority for temporary entry permission. Permission requires approval and must not be promised.

Scope: 24-hour transit; departure from port restricted area. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [中国驻多伦多总领事馆](https://toronto.china-consulate.gov.cn/chn/lsfw/HQGR/50/202407/t20240709_11451153.htm)

## Coverage limits
The 2025 NIA annex retains an older nationality count; use the newer 2026 consular list for the 57-country roster. A nationwide unilateral waiver, bilateral agreement and 240-hour transit are separate schemes. Nationality alone is insufficient.
