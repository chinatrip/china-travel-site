# Residence permits and extensions in China

## When must I apply for a residence permit after entry?

If your visa specifies that a residence permit is required after entry, apply to the public-security exit and entry administration at the intended place of residence within 30 days of entry.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html) · [en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## When should I apply to extend a residence permit?

Plan to apply 30 days before expiry under the Chinese text of Article 32. Check the documents and appointment arrangements with the exit and entry administration where you live.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html) · [en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html) · [www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## What are the consequences of unlawful residence?

Article 78 provides for a warning. In serious cases the law provides for a fine of CNY 500 per day, up to CNY 10,000, or detention for 5–15 days.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html) · [en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## Which residence permit matches my purpose?

The five categories are work, long-term study, resident journalism, family reunion and private affairs. Reunion covers qualifying relatives of Chinese citizens or of foreigners with permanent residence in China, as well as foster care. Certain relatives visiting foreigners who reside in China for work or study may qualify under private affairs.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155970/content.html)

## What documents should I take for a residence permit?

Prepare your passport or other international travel document, compliant photos and evidence for the purpose of residence. Article 16 lists supporting materials by category, such as a work permit for an ordinary work application or a letter from the admitting institution stating the duration of study. The usual procedure includes applying in person and providing biometrics.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155970/content.html)

## What can I use while the authority holds my passport?

Once the residence-permit application is accepted, the authority issues an acceptance receipt valid for up to 15 days and decides within its validity. While it holds the passport or other travel document for this application, the receipt allows the applicant to reside lawfully in China.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155970/content.html)

## Which deadlines apply to a visa extension or a change of residence-permit details?

To extend the stay allowed by a visa, apply at least seven days before that stay expires. If a registered detail on a residence permit changes, such as the passport number, apply to change the permit within ten days of the change.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## When does a residence permit application require a health certificate?

Article 16 sets a six-month validity period for the health certificate, counted from its issue date. For the permit length that triggers this requirement, the Chinese regulation says “1年以上”. If you are applying for exactly one year, check the receiving exit-entry office’s requirements before your appointment. For a longer permit, prepare the certificate in accordance with the applicable rules.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155970/content.html) · [www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741587/n1316094/n1355872/c1356497/content.html)

## What identity document must I carry while staying in China?

Foreign nationals aged 16 or older who stay or reside in China must carry their passport or another international travel document, or their foreigner’s stay or residence permit, for public-security inspections.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## Which photo does the government’s residence-permit guide specify?

For a residence-permit application, the government guide lists a completed Foreigner Visa Application Form and a recent, bareheaded, 33 × 48 mm colour identification photo against a white background. Check the receiving office’s instructions for submitting your materials.

[english.www.gov.cn](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025/)

## What family documents does the NIA list for a reunion residence permit?

For a Q1 visa holder, the National Immigration Administration lists the identity document of the person being visited and a letter explaining the family relationship. A visitor holding another visa type must also provide proof of the family relationship.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741445/n741604/n1787224/n1795789/c1792630/content.html)

## Is Chinese permanent residence a separate application?

Yes. The National Immigration Administration publishes separate permanent residence approval rules. An applicant must meet one of the qualifying categories and satisfy the stated conditions.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1295837/content.html)

## Travel tip — Preparing an appointment

Make a checklist from the issuing office’s instructions for your category. Save the appointment, office address and document checklist together.
