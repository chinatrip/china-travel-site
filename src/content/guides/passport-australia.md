# Australia 澳大利亚 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## What should I prepare for a new adult passport?
Prepare original citizenship and identity documents, two compliant photos and a qualifying referee or guarantor. The choice between referee and guarantor depends on the application method.

Scope: Australian citizens applying for a new adult passport inside Australia. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [澳大利亚外交贸易部 Australian Passport Office](https://www.passports.gov.au/adult-passport)

## Does completing the online form finish the application?
Completing the form online does not complete lodgement: print it and lodge at a participating post office with the required originals. Allow at least six weeks; overseas applications and renewals have separate instructions.

Scope: Australian citizens applying for a new adult passport inside Australia. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [澳大利亚外交贸易部 Australian Passport Office](https://www.passports.gov.au/adult-passport)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
