# Australia Passport: Application Preparation

## What should I prepare for a new adult passport?

Prepare original citizenship and identity documents, two compliant photos and a qualifying referee or guarantor. The choice between referee and guarantor depends on the application method.

Scope: Australian citizens applying for a new adult passport inside Australia

[www.passports.gov.au](https://www.passports.gov.au/adult-passport)

## Does completing the online form finish the application?

Completing the form online does not complete lodgement: print it and lodge at a participating post office with the required originals. Allow at least six weeks; overseas applications and renewals have separate instructions.

Scope: Australian citizens applying for a new adult passport inside Australia

[www.passports.gov.au](https://www.passports.gov.au/adult-passport)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

Australia’s travel advisory warns that internet restrictions can affect communication and access to online banking. Arrange a backup way to communicate.

Scope: Advice for travellers from Australia.

[www.smartraveller.gov.au](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/china)
