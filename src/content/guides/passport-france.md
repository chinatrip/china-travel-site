# France Passport: Application Preparation

## Where do I apply for a first adult passport?

An equipped mairie can accept the application regardless of the applicant’s home municipality; many require appointments. The applicant must attend for fingerprints. An online pre-application is optional and does not replace attendance.

Scope: French citizens, first adult passport application in France

[www.service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14929)

## What if I hold a valid French identity card?

For an applicant with a valid national identity card, the guide lists that card, a compliant photo less than six months old and address evidence less than one year old. Different identity-document situations have separate checklists. Check the full current list, including the required fiscal stamp; these examples cover only part of the application.

Scope: French citizens, first adult passport application in France

[www.service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14929)

## What should I prepare for the trip to China?

France advises checking entry formalities with the Chinese authorities. If transiting Southeast Asia, pay particular attention to the condition of your passport.

Scope: Advice for travellers from France.

[www.diplomatie.gouv.fr](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/information-par-pays/chine/conseils-aux-voyageurs-entree-sejour)
