# France 法国 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## Where do I apply for a first adult passport?
An equipped mairie can accept the application regardless of the applicant’s home municipality; many require appointments. The applicant must attend for fingerprints. An online pre-application is optional and does not replace attendance.

Scope: French citizens, first adult passport application in France. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [法国 DILA / 内政部，Service Public](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14929)

## What if I hold a valid French identity card?
For an applicant with a valid national identity card, the guide lists that card, a compliant photo less than six months old and address evidence less than one year old. Different identity-document situations have separate checklists.

Scope: French citizens, first adult passport application in France. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [法国 DILA / 内政部，Service Public](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F14929)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
