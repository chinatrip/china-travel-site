# Foreign work permits in China

## Are points the only way to qualify for a foreign work permit?

The published trial classification standard provides several routes. The points routes list at least 85 points for Category A and at least 60 for Category B. Ask the competent authority which route and documentation apply to the position.

[fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## When must I apply for a residence permit after entry?

If your visa specifies that a residence permit is required after entry, apply to the public-security exit and entry administration at the intended place of residence within 30 days of entry.

[www.nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html) · [en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## What does the law say about unlawful employment?

Article 80 sets a fine of CNY 5,000–20,000 for foreigners who work illegally. In serious cases it provides for 5–15 days of detention together with the fine.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## Who handles a work permit extension?

The Ministry of Commerce guide directs employers to apply to the competent provincial or municipal human resources and social security department for a work permit extension. Check the permit’s expiry and local submission procedure with the employer. Residence permits have a separate extension procedure.

[www.mofcom.gov.cn](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/ztxx/art/2026/art_90fd4865144d4ddab192742bf386bbd1.html)

## Is any job allowed once I have a work permit?

For employment covered by the law, Article 41 requires a work permit and a work-type residence permit. Article 43 includes working beyond the scope of the work permit as unlawful employment. It also covers foreign students whose work-study activity breaches the permitted duties or hours.

[en.nia.gov.cn](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147458/c155978/content.html)

## How are work experience and salary counted on the work-permit points route?

In Shanghai’s guide, two qualifying years of experience earn five points, with one extra point per additional year, up to 20. Supply records from former employers for the years claimed. Salary is a separate item worth up to 20 points, reached at CNY 450,000 or more in annual pay from the employer in China. Other eligibility routes have different criteria.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-TalentsinShanghai/20260813/52568cc4e18c4c4cb45037ec81dab201.html)

## Which evidence earns five Chinese-language points on the work-permit points route?

Shanghai’s guide lists previous Chinese nationality, a bachelor’s degree or higher taught in Chinese, or HSK level 5 or above for five language points. HSK levels 1 through 4 earn the corresponding one to four points. Provide documentation for the basis you claim.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-TalentsinShanghai/20260813/52568cc4e18c4c4cb45037ec81dab201.html)

## Which professional achievements can qualify under the Category A work-permit standard?

The national trial standard lists several routes. Examples include selection for a qualifying talent programme, membership of a national academy of sciences or engineering, or a Nobel Prize in physics, chemistry, physiology or medicine, or economics. The research-publication criterion specifies three papers as first or corresponding author, including equal contributions, in JCR first- or second-quartile journals in the field. The authority assesses the application.

[fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## Does every Category B foreign worker need a degree and two years’ experience?

That is one route for specified professional roles, subject to the catalogue and position requirements. Other routes in the national trial standard include a qualifying vocational certificate or scarce skill, specified project personnel, and average pay of at least four times the previous year’s local average wage. For foreign-language teaching, the standard provides exceptions to the two-year teaching-experience requirement for specified degrees or teaching credentials. Check which route your employer is using.

[fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## What does Category C cover in the national foreign-worker standard?

The classification includes temporary or short-term work of up to 90 days and certain quota-managed groups, such as young interns under government agreements. Short duration alone does not give permission to work. Confirm the required authorisation for the activity before starting.

[fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## Can I add points for several degrees or several extra qualifications?

Shanghai’s guide gives 10 points for a bachelor’s degree, 15 for a master’s and 20 for a doctorate. Only the highest applicable degree counts. A separate item for specified qualifications, including qualifying patents or experience at a Fortune Global 500 company, is capped at five points altogether. Prepare the evidence for each item you claim.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-TalentsinShanghai/20260813/52568cc4e18c4c4cb45037ec81dab201.html) · [fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## Does the foreign-worker points table account for age, months worked or local incentives?

The trial table assigns 15 age points for ages 26–45 and zero above 60. Working at least nine months a year earns 15 points under the time criterion, while fewer than three months earns zero. The location item includes designated areas in the west and in older industrial bases such as the northeast. A separate local incentive can add up to 10 points under provincial criteria.

[fuwu.most.gov.cn](https://fuwu.most.gov.cn/r/cms/zwpt/web/pdf/wgrlhzq/20180731103648_983.pdf)

## Which research or university roles are recognised under the Category A work-permit standard?

Published examples include directors or senior researchers at national research institutes or laboratories, chief scientists on national science and technology programmes, and former professors or associate professors at qualifying high-level universities outside the Chinese mainland. The authority checks the role and institution against the classification criteria.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Employment-EligibilityandWorkpermit/20260813/3b14d60fe3484535958e580a24720412.html) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/government/policytoolkittwo/INV_Policy_Text/202411/t20241118_3944100.html)

## Which contracts and representative-office roles appear in the Category B standard?

One Category B route for professionals with a bachelor’s degree or higher and at least two years of relevant experience covers people implementing specified intergovernmental agreements or Sino-foreign trade, engineering or technical contracts. The same route lists staff of international organisations’ representative offices in China and chief representatives or representatives of foreign businesses’ resident offices. Check the applicable position and catalogue requirements.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Employment-EligibilityandWorkpermit/20260813/3b14d60fe3484535958e580a24720412.html)

## Who counts as outstanding young talent under the Category A trial standard?

This route covers people under 40 doing postdoctoral research at a high-level university outside the Chinese mainland or at a university on the Chinese mainland. A university degree alone does not meet this criterion.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Employment-EligibilityandWorkpermit/20260813/3b14d60fe3484535958e580a24720412.html)

## Which science awards and international roles appear in the Category A trial standard?

The listed awards include the Turing Award, Fields Medal, Wolf Prize, Abel Prize, Lasker Award, Kyoto Prize and Shaw Prize. The standard also lists specified membership or council roles in internationally recognised institutions such as ISO. It separately lists senior management or key research and development roles at Global 500 headquarters and certain senior roles in their subsidiaries or regional headquarters.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-Employment-EligibilityandWorkpermit/20260813/3b14d60fe3484535958e580a24720412.html) · [english.beijing.gov.cn](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/government/policytoolkittwo/INV_Policy_Text/202411/t20241118_3944100.html)

## What travel, photo and background documents does Shanghai list for a work-permit notification?

Shanghai’s notification-letter guide lists a passport or international travel document, a front-facing bareheaded photograph taken within six months, a certificate of no criminal record with its certification documents, and a physical-examination certificate. The full checklist also covers employment and qualifications. Use the checklist for your application route.

[english.shanghai.gov.cn](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-application/20231219/c8e2ea368ecf47dc98155d06d00ac0be.html)

## Travel tip — Keeping track of an application

Ask the employer which office is handling your case. Save the application reference, copies and written follow-up instructions. Work from the dates on your own notice.
