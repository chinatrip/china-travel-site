# Tibet Travel Permits and Altitude Preparation

A China visa or visa exemption does not by itself settle permission to visit 西藏 (Tibet). Arrange your itinerary with a specialist operator and check the required permissions before committing to non-refundable travel.

## Can I visit Tibet independently on a China visa?

The [UK government regional advice for British travellers](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/china/regional-risks) says Tibet travel requires a permit arranged through a specialist travel agent in China and an organised tour. It warns that permits or travel can be restricted without notice. Travellers of other nationalities should confirm their own consular guidance and route requirements with the responsible authorities and operator; do not infer an exception from a visa-free nationality list.

Ask the operator for written confirmation of the itinerary, required permits, approved dates, delivery arrangements, total price and cancellation terms. This guide does not guarantee processing days, a permit price, approval, or an annually fixed closure period.

## Is the border-area permit the same as the Tibet travel permit?

No: the **边境管理区通行证 (Border Administration Area Permit)** is a separate permission. NIA introduced its electronic form on **15 April 2026**. The notice says foreign nationals apply **in person** at the relevant public-security exit-entry administration or designated police station; the mainland-resident online process is not a general foreign-tourist self-service route. The electronic document is used with valid identity documentation and can be shown on a device or as a printout. Ask which permissions your precise route requires. Source: [NIA electronic border permit announcement](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147468/c199270/content.html).

## What is known about the Shigatse restriction?

An NIA notice dated **27 August 2026**, published the next day, suspended issuance of electronic border-area permits for 日喀则 (Shigatse) from **28 August 2026 at 00:00** after the disaster near 吉隆 (Gyirong). It also told holders of already-issued permits naming Shigatse not to go, with resumption to be announced separately. Source: [NIA suspension notice](https://www.nia.gov.cn/n897453/c1797217/content.html).

**Current route availability remains unconfirmed in this review.** The dated notice is not proof that every part of Tibet is closed today, nor proof that a route is open. Confirm the latest permit and road status with 12367 and your operator before departure; do not promise access to Everest or a Nepal crossing on the basis of an old itinerary.

## What if I enter from Nepal?

Confirm both the border route and applicable visa/permit process with the Chinese embassy in Nepal and your specialist operator. Its [ordinary-visa instructions](https://np.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/ConsularServices/cv/202505/t20250529_11636825.htm) list a Group L visa with an invitation from an authorized tourism unit. That does not establish that every traveller of every nationality must replace an existing visa, or that a land crossing is operating. Those blanket claims have not been substantiated here. Obtain instructions for your specific passport, group and itinerary before paying.

## How should I prepare for altitude?

Plan gradual ascent and a medical contingency with a clinician and operator. Do not continue to a higher sleeping altitude while unwell. **Breathlessness at rest, confusion or difficulty walking require urgent help and safe descent**, not a reassurance that they are ordinary acclimatization. Avoid relying on small oxygen cans for serious illness. Source: [CDC altitude guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html).

Rhodiola (红景天) is not an evidence-established preventive treatment. NIH notes side effects and a reported **losartan** interaction. Discuss medicines with a clinician; this guide gives no universal preventive dose or seven-day customs threshold. Source: [NIH NCCIH](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/rhodiola).

Ask your insurer to confirm coverage for the route, altitude, activities and emergency evacuation, and how to get assistance. Read [[wiki/concepts/health-medical.md]] for emergency care and medicines and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for China entry routes.

## How high is Lhasa, and how should I plan the first days at altitude?

Lhasa (拉萨) is about 3,650 metres above sea level. CDC recommends a gradual ascent where possible. For at least the first 48 hours above 8,000 feet (about 2,440 metres), avoid alcohol and strenuous exercise. If altitude symptoms develop, wait until they resolve before going higher.

[www.cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/asia/china.html) · [wwwnc.cdc.gov](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/travel-to-high-altitudes/)

## Can acetazolamide help me adjust to altitude?

Acetazolamide can help the body acclimatize faster. The CDC bases prevention advice on your ascent, sleeping altitude and prior experience at altitude. Discuss your route and whether the medicine is appropriate for you with a clinician before travelling.

[www.cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html)

## Can a foreign tourist travel independently in the Tibet Autonomous Region?

Shannan’s municipal guidance tells foreign tourists visiting the Tibet Autonomous Region to join a tour organised by a qualified travel agency. The agency applies for the Tibet travel approval letter (入藏函) on their behalf. Check your exact route with the agency because border-area documentation is a separate step.

[www.shannan.gov.cn](https://www.shannan.gov.cn/zjsn/snly/jzcs/202609/t20260918_176895.html)

## Does the Tibet travel letter rule differ for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan residents?

Shannan’s municipal guide says Hong Kong and Macao residents entering Tibet with a Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Residents (回乡证) do not need the Tibet travel letter. It directs Taiwan residents to use a qualified travel agency and supply a scan of their Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents (台胞证).

[www.shannan.gov.cn](https://www.shannan.gov.cn/zjsn/snly/jzcs/202609/t20260918_176895.html)

## Which Shigatse counties does Shannan list as border areas?

Shannan’s border-permit guide lists Yadong (亚东) and Tingri (定日) among the border counties in Shigatse (日喀则). A county’s inclusion on that list does not establish that a particular road, crossing or visitor route is open. Check the current route before booking.

[www.shannan.gov.cn](https://www.shannan.gov.cn/zjsn/snly/jzcs/202512/t20251229_162221.html) · [www.shannan.gov.cn](https://www.shannan.gov.cn/zjsn/snly/jzcs/202609/t20260918_176895.html)

## When did Gyirong Port reopen after the July 2025 flood?

The Tibet regional government records that flooding interrupted clearance at Gyirong Port (吉隆口岸) on 8 July 2025. Passenger and cargo clearance resumed on 1 January 2026. Check current border notices separately before booking.

[www.xizang.gov.cn](https://www.xizang.gov.cn/xwzx_406/bmkx/202601/t20260102_517200.html)

## Travel tip — Planning before paying a deposit

Tell your operator the exact route, dates and entry point, including any land border. Ask for written confirmation of arrangements and cancellation terms. Discuss your own high-altitude itinerary with a travel clinician.
