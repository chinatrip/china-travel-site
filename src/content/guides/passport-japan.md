# Japan 日本 passport application before China travel

Government-source baseline for local passport preparation; this does not determine permission to enter China.

## What documents does a first paper application require?
Paper applications require the form, a family-register transcript issued within six months, a compliant 45×35 mm photo taken within six months, and original identity evidence. A residence certificate is required only in specified circumstances.

Scope: Japan citizens applying domestically; first passport using paper application. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [日本外务省](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/toko/passport/pass_2.html)

## Where can I apply and collect my passport?
Applications are handled by prefectural passport offices in Japan and diplomatic missions abroad. Online first applications and renewals are also available, subject to channel eligibility. Collection requires the applicant to attend.

Scope: Japan citizens applying domestically; first passport using paper application. Checked: 2026-11-17.
Sources: [日本外务省](https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/toko/passport/pass_2.html)

## Coverage limits
Not a complete checklist for every age, citizenship history, residence, renewal or emergency case. Passport issuance and China entry eligibility are separate questions.

## China entry requirements

A passport alone does not establish permission to enter China. See [[wiki/concepts/china-entry-country-rules.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/visa-types.md]] for separate nationality, purpose and route checks.
